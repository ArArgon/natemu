=== el1_features: what a guest at EL1 under Hypervisor.framework can ACTUALLY do ===
    every probe runs in its own forked VM; the vCPU is put in EL1h (CPSR=0x3c5) before
    its first MRS, and VBAR_EL1 points at our own vector table so an undef is survivable

=== 6. TLBI at guest EL1 =======================================================
  semantic test: cache a level-3 entry, demote the leaf to read-only behind the TLB,
  invalidate with ONE variant, then store again. FAULT = the variant really invalidated.
    TLBI (no TLBI)              not invalidated (stale entry used) 
    TLBI VMALLE1                INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VMALLE1IS              INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VAE1                   INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VAE1IS                 INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VAAE1                  INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VAAE1IS                INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VALE1                  INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VALE1IS                INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI ASIDE1                 INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI ASIDE1IS               INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI ASIDE1IS wrong ASID    not invalidated (stale entry used) 
    TLBI VAE1IS wrong VA        not invalidated (stale entry used) 
    TLBI RVAE1IS (TLBIRANGE)    INVALIDATED (store faulted)        
    TLBI VMALLE1OS (TLBIOS)     INVALIDATED (store faulted)
