# Research-phase executor guidance

Read [the research workflow](docs/research/README.md) first, then your assigned
[feasibility charter](docs/research/tracks/01-feasibility.md). The workflow governs
new work; each investigation owns its current conclusion and next action.

Use subagents by default for independent research reviews and replication;
keep authors and reviewers separate.

Do not read or search git history for the archived Track 01 material unless the
task names it.
