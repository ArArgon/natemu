## Question and decision relevance

- Question: `Q-01-23` — What preemption, QoS, P/E migration, and
  oversubscription contract does the host provide at 2/4/8 vCPUs, and which
  guest-visible lock-holder, soft-lockup, RCU, or spin-wait failures can result?
- Decisions affected: SMP and timer feasibility, quiescence, correctness
  cell-pass rules, placement normalization, and family admissibility.
- Why now: a correct vCPU can be descheduled while holding guest/protocol state;
  the resulting warning or stall counts as a cell failure and is not answered
  by memory-order or timer primitives alone.

## Assumptions

- Declared: 2/4/8 product vCPUs; P1/P2 separate; placement claims require valid
  instrumentation; correctness failures suppress performance samples.
- Challenge: QoS pins core type; affinity tags control placement; vCPU count
  below total cores avoids oversubscription; host fairness bounds lock-holder
  preemption; yielding spin loops preserve guest semantics; steady-state tests
  cover background and thermal transitions.

  stall, spin-wait starvation, lost quiescence acknowledgement, or timer/event
  failure in every retained discipline for a required cell.

## Controls

- Positive: prove every vCPU ran, held/released the target lock/state, and
  reached quiescence; independently observe timer and harness progress.
- Negative: forced lock-holder deschedule, missing yield, stale acknowledgement,
  lost wakeup, falsified placement sample, broken ordering, and frozen vCPU must
  each fail the intended oracle.
- Baselines: no oversubscription, each QoS class, yield/spin/park variants, and
  1-thread diagnostic; none substitutes for 2/4/8 product cells.
