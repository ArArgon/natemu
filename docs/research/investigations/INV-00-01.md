## Question and decision relevance

- Question: `Q-00-01` — Which intended user classes and concrete, diverse
  workloads must the emulator represent?

  performance aggregation, economics, and synthesis applicability.
- Why now: mechanisms and tests must not select the workload they favor.

  and direct boot are baseline.
- Challenged: generic amd64-container demand implies need for a full VM;
  compilation alone is representative; elapsed time always has labor value.

## Resolution

### Users

1. **U1 — provisional x86-64 Linux kernel and low-level system-software
   maintainers on Apple Silicon:** candidate job is to build, direct-boot,
   debug, and regression-test an unmodified x86-64 kernel/userspace with SMP
   and guest devices.
2. **U2 — provisional distribution/package integration and CI maintainers
   requiring machine isolation:** candidate job is to install packages in a
   disposable x86-64 VM and test services, reboots, kernel-facing behavior,
   networking, and storage.
3. **U3 — amd64 application/container builders, conditional:** included only
   where per-workflow evidence rules out correctness-equivalent
   cross-compilation, user-mode emulation, native x86 builders, and remote CI.
4. **U4 — x86-only service-stack developers, conditional:** included only when
   per-workflow evidence establishes that a distinct x86 kernel/system
   environment is required.

These are scope candidates, not an observed target population. U1 and U2 have
zero population, adoption, sponsorship, and economic weight until `Q-06-02`
passes independent review. U3 and U4 additionally have zero success weight
until full-system necessity and alternative equivalence pass for the exact
workflow. Upstream workflow documentation cannot satisfy either condition.

GUI/desktop use, gaming, Windows execution, production VM hosting, and
single-binary legacy execution are not target uses. `Q-00-09` covers Windows
implications only.

Diagnostic-only controls are immutable fio jobs (`D-FIO`), iperf3 host/guest
network tests (`D-NET`), selected kselftests (`D-KSELF`), and Track 02
ISA/exception/SMP suites. They have zero success and economic weight.

`Q-05-03` must give every row an immutable manifest containing source commit or
release plus content digest; guest/base-image and package-snapshot digests;
exact package/test/script selections; build/runtime configuration and
toolchain; seed/data set; cache warm/cold preparation; storage and network
configuration; client/helper placement; reset procedure; per-phase timeout;
expected output, failure, retry, and skip policy; negative cases; and artifact
retention paths. “Pinned,” “preregistered,” or “frozen” is not satisfied until
that reviewed manifest exists. Any manifest change creates a new workload
revision and invalidates cross-revision aggregation unless equivalence is
separately established.

For every measured cell, record guest/CPU/memory, APIC and exposed vCPU
topology, host model and available P/E cores, requested and observed vCPU and
emulator/helper-thread placement, oversubscription, thermals/power policy,
client location, concurrent VM/CI/host load, storage/network backend, cache and
reset proof, and correctness outcome. Two- and four-vCPU rows are the minimum
mandatory cells; one vCPU is diagnostic. Eight vCPUs is required only where the
accepted product or platform claim includes it and the host has an approved
non-oversubscribed placement. An omitted 8-vCPU cell is an explicit limitation,
not evidence at eight vCPUs. Any topology exception must be preregistered in
`Q-05-03`, justified by the workflow, and excluded from broader scaling claims.
`Q-05-02` and `Q-05-06` must pass before cross-platform, topology-wide, or
cost-equivalence generalization.

### Inclusion, aggregation, and alternative rule

- The primary portfolio rule is a non-compensating conjunction: after
  `Q-00-04` sets workload-specific thresholds, every admitted mandatory
  workload must pass every required exact cell. Report the per-workload vector;
  a strong build result cannot offset a boot, system, or package-CI failure.
  No scalar weighted score is authorized now. If `Q-00-04` later requires one,
  it must preregister weights before measurements, make every mandatory
  workload weight positive, make weights sum to one, and publish sensitivity;
  until that review passes all weights and the aggregate are undefined.
- `W-OCI`, `W-PG`, diagnostics, failed/ineligible cells, and unrepresented
  platforms have zero success and economic weight. A conditional workload may
  enter only after `Q-06-02` establishes recurring full-system necessity for
  that workflow, the same corpus/correctness prerequisites pass, and
  `Q-00-04` assigns a weight without retrospectively selecting favorable data.
- No single workload, arithmetic mean, compilation-only result, or 2/4-vCPU
  result represents the portfolio, eight-vCPU behavior, or all platforms.
- Before full-system necessity or benefit is claimed for a workload,
  `Q-06-02` and `Q-05-04` must document the organization-specific
  ladder: current workflow; same-semantics local full-system baseline;
  user-mode emulation; cross-compilation; native local or owned x86 hardware;
  native/remote CI; and on-demand hosted compute. Compare the same accepted
  output and security/access constraints, guest and inputs, queueing,
  concurrency, transfer/egress, region/plan, vCPU/RAM/storage/network,
  administration, energy, and allocated sunk hardware. Reject an alternative
  only with matched evidence. A feasible correctness-equivalent substitute is
  a per-workload falsifier, never a blanket conclusion about other users.

## Hypothesis and falsifier

- Hypothesis: the contract captures the minimum decision-relevant full-system
  jobs without converting generic cross-platform demand into an adoption claim.
- Falsifiers: `Q-06-02` finds no recurring U1/U2 cohort needing a full VM; a
  candidate or conditional job has a feasible correctness-equivalent
  alternative; the mandatory set cannot pass its immutable oracle and accepted
  Track 02 surface on every claimed guest/platform at 2/4 vCPUs; an 8-vCPU claim
  lacks its exact cell; the set lacks block, network, system, or SMP behavior;
  or inputs, exclusions, or weights are selected after results.
- Predicted: `Q-06-02` may narrow or eliminate candidate users; `Q-05-03` may
  reject workloads whose inputs or oracles cannot be made immutable.

## Method, controls, metrics, and limitations

- Procedure: inspect primary upstream full-system workflows and alternatives;
  derive a portfolio covering every product function/workload class; challenge
  it against substitutes and oracle availability.
- Positive controls: documented full-system and upstream VM test workflows.
  Negative controls: user-mode/container-only and native arm64 work.
- Baselines: owned by `Q-00-03`.
- Metrics: candidate definitions appear above; their final operationalization
  and aggregation are `Q-05-03` and `Q-00-04` outputs.
- Sample plan, exclusions, code validation, raw artifacts, results, and
  correctness outcome: not applicable because this investigation runs no
  experiment.
- Limitations: target-scope proposal, not population or economic research;
  U1/U2 are provisional; `W-OCI`/`W-PG` remain conditional and zero-weight;
  exact corpora and oracles belong to `Q-05-03`, and only later accepted
  evidence can make a row representative.
