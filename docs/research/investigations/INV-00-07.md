## Resolution

### Valuation convention and accounting model

The valuation date `t0` is the date of the first authorized development
expenditure. The horizon ends five years later at `t5`. `y=0` is the `t0`
cash-flow bucket and `y=1..5` are the five annual end-of-period buckets, all in
constant currency of `t0`; each input records its actual
transaction/observation date and the preregistered convention used to place it
at a boundary. The proposed real discount rate is `r = 10%`. It is not an
empirical estimate and cannot be used until a named Track 00 decision owner
approves it before input collection.

```text
PVC = sum[y=0..5] (
        C_development,y + C_maintenance,y + C_release,y + C_support,y
        + C_validation,y + C_incident,y + C_CI,y + C_distribution,y
        + C_hardware,y + C_admin,y
      ) / (1+r)^y
      - RV_hardware,5 / (1+r)^5

PVB = sum[o,w,y=1..5] I[w] * N[o,w,y] * D[o,w,y] * F[o,w,y]
      * E[o,w,y]
      * (B_labor[o,w,y] + B_schedule[o,w,y]
         + B_compute[o,w,y] + B_license[o,w,y]
         + B_admin[o,w,y] + B_hardware[o,w,y])
      / (1+r)^y

B_labor[o,w,y] = A[o,w,y] * deltaT[o,w,y] * V[o,w,y]
```

`PVC` is the project-attributable lifecycle cost. Hardware purchase/lease,
operations, energy, depreciation, and shared test/CI capacity are allocated once
under a reviewed usage rule; existing hardware is not free. A terminal residual
or redeployable hardware value `RV_hardware,5` requires independent support, is
capped at the allocated acquisition value, and is zero when unsupported.

### Reviewed-input specification required before calculation

`Q-05-04`, `Q-06-02`, and `Q-06-03` must publish a method-reviewed data
dictionary and immutable input table with organization/workflow/year keys,
source artifact, collector, collection date and interval, denominator,
inclusion/exclusion reason, currency/base date, and reviewer. At minimum it
must implement these definitions:

| Input | Required meaning and provenance | Exclusions and zero rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `o`, `w`, `y` | de-duplicated organization, accepted workload revision, and valuation period; common-control entities are linked | the same execution, sponsor, or beneficiary cannot occur under multiple identities |
| `I[w]` | preregistered inclusion indicator | mandatory workloads remain zero until exact-cell prerequisites pass; conditional/diagnostic workloads are zero until their specific admission rule passes |
| `N[o,w,y]` | count of unique eligible accounting units with recorded sampling frame and denominator; a shared organization CI queue is one unit unless independently partitioned | no multiplication of one shared run by viewers, developers, repositories, or sponsors |
| `D[o,w,y]` | independently observed or contractually committed adoption share in `[0,1]`, separate from execution success | intention, source download, provider availability, and uncommitted interest are zero |
| `F[o,w,y]` | unique attempted executions per adopted accounting unit in the stated interval, including cancellations and retries | duplicate/replayed telemetry is excluded and logged; shared CI executions count once |
| `E[o,w,y]` | fraction of those attempts that produced an accepted, correctness-eligible output under the exact manifest/cell | failed, timed-out, cancelled, ineligible, or oracle-rejected attempts produce zero benefit, although their project costs remain in `PVC` |
| `A[o,w,y]` | measured active, non-parallel wait fraction in `[0,1]` with an activity-attribution protocol | unattended or overlapping work is zero labor time; it may enter a separate schedule account only with distinct evidence |
| `deltaT[o,w,y]` | accepted active-time difference per eligible execution against the cheapest correctness-equivalent alternative selected before measurement | unmatched topology/platform, guest, input, outcome, queue, or client placement is ineligible |
| `V[o,w,y]` | documented organization/population/period-specific fully loaded real hourly value and cost composition | neither BLS anchor is a project value by itself; do not combine the software wage with the all-private-industry benefit ratio or apply either without matched population, geography, period, composition, and attribution |
| `B_schedule` | separately evidenced value of an outcome delivery-date change, using a preregistered decision model | excludes every minute and dollar in labor or other benefit accounts; generic elapsed time is zero |
| `B_compute`, `B_license`, `B_admin`, `B_hardware` | net avoided metered/invoiced or reviewed allocated cost per accepted execution versus the selected alternative | mutually exclusive chart of accounts; sunk assets, depreciation, energy, administrator time, shared capacity, and residual value cannot be counted twice |

Adoption (`D`) and correctness-eligible realization (`E`) are distinct.
Reliability is not another multiplicative factor: failed/cancelled attempts are
already in the `E` denominator and their operating/incident costs enter `PVC`.
A standalone reliability or availability benefit is permitted only as a
preregistered non-overlapping schedule or avoided-cost outcome. Missing benefit
inputs are zero. A missing required cost, denominator, equivalence match, or
provenance field makes the model incomplete and therefore unable to pass.

### Alternative-equivalence rule

Before measuring `deltaT` or any avoided-cost account, each organization and
workload must record this ladder: its current workflow; the accepted
same-semantics full-system baseline; user-mode emulation; cross-compilation;
native local or owned x86 hardware; native/remote CI; hosted builder; and
on-demand hosted compute. The cheapest feasible alternative is selected only
after matching accepted output and correctness, guest/input, vCPU and topology,
RAM, storage, network, client placement, concurrent load, queueing, transfer and
egress, region and plan, access/security constraints, availability, support,
administration, energy, and allocated sunk hardware. Provider existence or list
price alone proves neither access nor equivalence. Rejection of an alternative
requires auditable per-field evidence; a feasible cheaper
correctness-equivalent alternative falsifies that workload's claimed benefit,
not every other workflow.

`Q-00-02`, `Q-05-03`, and accepted Track 02 correctness gate the exact guest,
manifest, and measured cell. `Q-05-02` and `Q-05-06` gate topology/platform and
resource equivalence. No 2/4-vCPU or single-host result can value an 8-vCPU,
different-placement, or platform-wide claim.

### Credibility conditions

Economic credibility requires every condition:

1. Every admitted workload has independently reviewed recurring user evidence,
   full-system necessity, immutable corpus/oracle, exact-cell correctness, and
   matched alternative evidence. `Q-00-04` separately owns performance-portfolio
   weights. Economic aggregation uses de-duplicated observed volumes rather
   than substituting those weights. Mandatory workloads have `I=0` until their
   prerequisites pass; conditional U3/U4 workloads and diagnostics remain
   `I=0` until their per-workflow admission rule passes.
2. Against the cheapest correctness-equivalent alternative, the approved base
   case has `PVB/PVC >= 1.5`, `PVB-PVC > 0`, and discounted payback within three
   years of `t0`. Discounted payback is the earliest annual boundary at which
   cumulative discounted benefits minus costs is nonnegative and remains
   nonnegative through `y=5`; absent such a boundary, there is no payback.
3. The downside is a preregistered *joint* distribution or scenario preserving
   within-organization and within-workload correlations among adoption,
   utilization, time, reliability realization, benefits, costs, and sponsor
   withdrawal. With adequate independently sampled organizations, the reviewed
   method may use a hierarchical bootstrap or Monte Carlo and a predeclared
   lower joint quantile. It may not combine independent marginal P25 benefits
   and P75 costs. With sparse data, use deterministic lower-benefit/upper-cost
   bounds, set missing benefits to zero, and do not label the result a
   percentile. The resulting joint downside must have `PVB >= PVC`.
4. The 10% rate, 1.5 BCR, and three-year payback are normative policy choices,
   not source-derived estimates. A named decision owner must approve their
   rationale before data collection. Report sensitivity at minimum for real
   `r = {0%, 5%, 10%, 15%}`, BCR thresholds `{1.0, 1.5, 2.0}`, and discounted
   payback limits `{2, 3, 5}` years, plus timing and residual-value sensitivities.
5. Mutable prices are snapshotted with plan, region, SKU, currency, billing
   unit, rounding, storage/network/egress, concurrency, and access conditions
   and refreshed within 90 days of synthesis. Adoption, frequency, active wait,
   labor attribution, effort, and maintenance use fresh evidence, never
   inherited estimates or provider/source availability as a proxy.
6. One auditable sponsorship route passes, with all commitments and volumes
   independently checked and uncommitted amounts assigned zero:
   - **Internal:** a named budget owner commits the project-attributable
     five-year development and maintenance funding in writing and supplies
     de-duplicated observed volume. Concentration is disclosed. A full sponsor
     withdrawal scenario removes all uncommitted future funding and benefit and
     must either leave funded maintenance through an orderly stop or trigger a
     preregistered stop condition.
   - **External:** at least three organizations unaffiliated by common control,
     common beneficial ownership, or control by the same parent provide written
     independently verified volume/funding commitments; no organization or
     affiliated group exceeds 50% of projected benefit. Duplicate users/runs
     across organizations count once. Loss of the largest group is included in
     the joint downside, and five-year maintenance remains funded or the stop
     condition triggers.
   - **Public-good:** legally/administratively committed funds cover the joint
     downside lifecycle cost and five-year maintenance schedule. Restricted
     uses, revocation terms, and renewal dependencies are modeled; prospective
     or renewable-but-uncommitted awards count zero. The benefit tests still
     pass independently of the funding label.
7. A named accountable owner, reviewed staffing/coverage plan, and committed
   maintenance capacity exist for every period; unsupported volunteer effort
   counts zero.

The resolution accepts the rule, not a positive economic finding. Public prices
identify candidate cost channels only. They do not establish Apple-Silicon user
population, full-system necessity, access, alternative equivalence, adoption,
labor value, sponsorship, effort, or realized value. The BLS observations are
separate sensitivity anchors from different populations/measures and cannot be
combined into project compensation without the reviewed matching rule above.

## Falsifiers, applicability, and controls

- Falsifiers: base BCR below 1.5, non-positive NPV, payback over three years,
  negative joint-downside NPV, a cheaper correctness-equivalent alternative for
  a claimed workflow, benefit dominated by unattributed unattended time,
  double-counted/shared executions or benefit channels, an omitted lifecycle
  cost, unsupported residual value, sponsor withdrawal that leaves maintenance
  unfunded, no accountable owner, or reliance on failed correctness evidence.
- Stratify inputs by organization/accounting unit, workload revision,
  host/guest, vCPU topology and placement, alternative, client location,
  concurrent load, plan/region, and year. A single machine or organization
  cannot establish platform-wide value.
- Failed, cancelled, timed-out, ineligible, duplicate, or conditional-unadmitted
  work has zero benefit; its attributable costs are retained. Every exclusion
  is reason-coded and auditable.
- Mutable prices must be snapshotted and refreshed. BLS data remain separate
  sensitivity anchors, not project cost facts or a composable loaded wage.

## Method, metrics, sample plan, and limitations

- Procedure: after all prerequisites pass, enumerate lifecycle costs and
  non-overlapping benefit channels; compare with a preregistered
  correctness-equivalent alternative ladder; precommit the input dictionary,
  exclusions, base/downside construction, sensitivities, and sponsor tests;
  route unknown benefits to zero and missing required costs/equivalence to an
  incomplete, non-passing model.
- Metrics: NPV, `PVB/PVC`, payback, and downside NPV.
- Sample plan, exclusions, code validation, raw artifact, results, and
  correctness status: not applicable here; no experiment or collection occurs.
  Any later collector or calculator requires independent method and code review,
  including malformed/missing inputs, duplicates, failed oracles, zero/negative
  savings, allocation totals, correlated downside, and funding-withdrawal cases.
- Limitations: user population, adoption, effort, and active-wait observations
  remain open; alternative equivalence, sponsorship, and maintenance evidence
  remain open; current economic credibility is not established.
