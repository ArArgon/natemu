This phase does not implement non-Linux guests; 32-bit kernel/userspace
compatibility; BIOS/UEFI implementation; Windows boot/execution; graphical,
GPU, audio, camera, input, USB, Bluetooth, printing, or desktop integration;
passthrough; privileged bridged/TAP networking; suspend/hibernate, hotplug,
snapshots, migration, fault tolerance, or deterministic/cycle-accurate
execution; nested virtualization; PMU fidelity; SMM; confidential computing or
enclaves; Intel Macs, non-macOS hosts, iOS/iPadOS, or non-Apple-Silicon hosts;
private APIs, kernel extensions, or unavailable entitlements; packaging, App
Store publication, commercial support, planning, or production implementation.

Those implementation non-goals do not suppress feasibility research. Track 01
must still analyze supported distribution forms, signing/notarization,
entitlements, lifecycle burden, and whether a documented public privileged
service would be necessary. The target product requirement is routine guest
operation without root or an installed privileged helper; this is a
user-facing deployment boundary, not a selected process architecture. Studying
a helper is permitted, and evidence that no admissible family can meet the
boundary reopens this question rather than being discarded. Packaging and
distribution costs remain mandatory inputs to `Q-06-03`, and licensing or
entitlement feasibility remains mandatory in `Q-01-01`/`Q-01-11`.

Security beyond the `Q-00-08` instance-isolation and crash-containment contract
is deferred, including formal noninterference, multi-tenant certification,
complete malicious-guest resistance, speculative/side-channel equivalence,
confidentiality against an authorized host user or administrator, encrypted
storage/networking, Secure/measured boot, TPM attestation, supply-chain
certification, and host-tamper resistance. Minimum access control is not
deferred: a guest must still be denied ungranted host files, credentials, IPC,
devices, and endpoints.

UEFI and Windows receive only `Q-00-09` analysis. Out-of-envelope probes are
labelled diagnostics; they establish no compatibility or performance claim.
Any promotion reopens `Q-00-02`, this question, stop conditions, and affected
gates. A review must be able to classify every task/result as required,
diagnostic, analysis-only, planning-owned, or deferred.

## Method, controls, falsifiers, and limitations

- Method: map every neighboring feature against the governing product envelope
  and required definitions without choosing a mechanism.
- Positive control: direct-boot SMP Linux and scoped headless devices remain
  required. Negative controls: a UEFI boot or GUI probe remains diagnostic.
- Falsifier dispositions: an accepted-workload dependency reopens the affected
  boundary; an unclassifiable task is a method/scope failure; removal of a
  retained correctness or access-control property invalidates this resolution;
  and a family that uniquely requires a prohibited mechanism is killed only
  after `Q-01-01`/`Q-01-11` establish that fact. Alternative public process and
  isolation families remain open until `Q-01-02`/`Q-01-14` review rejects them.
- Host/OS/guest/SMP applicability: the exclusions are envelope-wide; narrow
  diagnostic results retain their exact applicability.
- Metrics, samples, code, raw artifacts, results, and correctness outcome: not
  applicable; this is a boundary decision. No implementation decision is made.
- Newly surfaced questions: the reviewer-discovered distribution and lifecycle
  work maps to existing `Q-01-01`, `Q-01-11`, and `Q-06-03`; no new issue ID is
  required.
