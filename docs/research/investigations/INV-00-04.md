## Resolution

This is a normative **candidate definition**, not an accepted statistical
method or a finding that the numerical floors are sufficient. It becomes
usable only after accepted `Q-00-01`, `Q-00-02`, `Q-00-03`, `Q-00-07`,
`Q-02-01`, `Q-02-02`, `Q-05-01`, `Q-05-02`, `Q-05-07`, and `Q-05-08`
outputs fix its inputs and validate its implementation.

### Finite denominator and weighting algebra

Before decision data, `Q-00-01`, `Q-00-02`, `Q-05-02`, and `Q-05-03` must
freeze a finite required-stratum set `S*`. A stratum is:

```text
user class × (host generation, macOS build, SDK) × guest
× CPU profile × vCPU topology/placement class
```

`S*` contains only combinations the accepted support matrix declares required;
it is not an assumed Cartesian product. For each `s` in `S*`, freeze the finite
mandatory workload-class set `K_s*`; for each `(s,k)`, freeze the finite cell
set `C_s,k*`, where a cell adds workload/input, accepted primary estimand, and
cold/warm state. Every mandatory cell appears exactly once. Conditional work
is absent until admitted by the accepted scope; once admitted it cannot be
dropped after results. An unavailable or unmeasured required cell remains in
the denominator as a coverage gap and blocks a breadth-wide pass.

For each cell, let `theta` be its precommitted primary estimand and define an
oriented positive ratio greater than one when the candidate is better:

```text
lower-is-better: r_c = theta_c(QEMU) / theta_c(candidate)
higher-is-better: r_c = theta_c(candidate) / theta_c(QEMU)
```

The default stratum weight is `q_s = 1/|S*|`. Within a stratum, each mandatory
workload class receives `1/|K_s*|`, and cells within that class receive
`1/|C_s,k*|`. Thus:

```text
G_s,k = exp(sum[c in C_s,k*] log(r_c) / |C_s,k*|)
G_s   = exp(sum[k in K_s*] log(G_s,k) / |K_s*|)
G     = exp(sum[s in S*] q_s * log(G_s))
```

This fixes aggregation for unequal numbers of workloads without rewarding a
class for adding benchmarks. Independently evidenced usage weights from
accepted `Q-00-07`/`Q-06-02` may replace only `q_s`; every proposed weight set
must be frozen before decision data, sum to one over the unchanged `S*`, state
its population and source, and receive independent review. Data-fitted weights
are prohibited.

The candidate “materially better” definition passes only if every condition
holds:

1. Every cell in frozen `C*` has an admitted comparable baseline and all
   applicable correctness and compatibility gates pass; a missing required
   cell cannot be treated as a pass.
2. The one-sided 95% lower confidence bound for the overall weighted
   geometric-mean speedup is at least **1.50×**.
3. For every finite `(s,k)` contrast, the simultaneous one-sided 95% lower
   bound on `G_s,k` is at least **1.25×**.
4. Every mandatory workload cell has a multiplicity-adjusted one-sided 95%
   lower bound of at least **0.909×**, which rules out more than a 10% increase
   in candidate elapsed time.
5. For peak RSS, host CPU-seconds, and host energy per correct completed unit,
   the portfolio one-sided 95% upper bound on the weighted
   `candidate/QEMU` ratio is at most **1.25×**; every mandatory-cell bound is
   at most **1.50×**. A metric may enter only after `Q-05-08` validates its
   instrument on the applicable platform; absent required instrumentation
   blocks success rather than silently dropping the guard. Portfolio resource
   ratios use the same frozen `q_s`, class, and cell weights as `G`.
6. Every run produces the accepted output. A wrong answer, hang, crash,
   isolation failure, or unexplained skip is a correctness failure rather than
   a slow or excludable sample.
7. Startup, steady-state throughput, fixed-load tail latency, or
   time-to-completion is primary only when Q-00-01 precommits that metric for
   the workload. A favorable metric may not be substituted afterward.

### Statistical-method requirements delegated to `Q-05-01`/`Q-05-07`

This investigation does not select an estimator, resampling algorithm,
replicate count, or multiplicity procedure. The downstream proposals must:

1. define the estimand for every metric, including means, throughput, and tail
   quantiles, and preserve the oriented-ratio direction;
2. model the actually present hierarchy (host session, VM boot, process, and
   iteration), pairing, autocorrelation, shared host/thermal state, and any
   unequal cluster sizes;
3. freeze four finite contrast families before data: the single overall `G`;
   all `(s,k)` class guards; all cells in `C* = union C_s,k*`; and all
   portfolio/cell resource guards by instrument;
4. specify coverage-preserving intervals and simultaneous-error control for
   those families, plus censoring, timeout, missing-block, and missing-cell
   handling;
5. determine repetition and stopping rules by preregistered power/precision
   analysis, not by an unsupported fixed minimum; and
6. demonstrate nominal coverage, family-wise error, and correct decision-zone
   classification with simulations spanning plausible hierarchy variance,
   autocorrelation, thermal drift, censored completion, failures, and missing
   or unbalanced cells before any decision run.

Balanced paired blocks, hierarchy-aware resampling, one-sided intervals, and
Bonferroni adjustment are candidate methods for `Q-05-01`/`Q-05-07` to test,
not approved choices here. Analysis code must fail closed on swapped ratio
direction, omitted strata, wrong-answer samples, invalid seeds, and incomplete
multiplicity families.

### Failure, censoring, and disposition rules

| Condition | Disposition |
| --- | --- |
| all finite contrasts and correctness/resource/economic guards pass | candidate performance success; synthesis authority is still absent |
| adequate valid data but a bound neither passes nor crosses an accepted kill boundary | inconclusive; never rounded into pass or kill |
| insufficient precision at the approved collection limit | inconclusive; additional work only through the accepted `INV-00-06` extension rule |
| wrong answer, crash, oracle-defined hang, isolation failure, or unexplained skip | correctness-ineligible; invalidate affected performance/resource/economic derivatives, not a censored slow sample |
| correctness-valid completion beyond an observation limit | right-censored only if the accepted `Q-05-07` method supports it; otherwise inconclusive, never replaced by the limit value |
| predeclared external interruption or instrument failure | exclude the complete affected paired block under the accepted method and report it; no one-sided sample deletion |
| missing or baseline-non-comparable required cell | coverage gap that blocks success and platform-wide claims; it is neither a measured regression nor permission to shrink `C*` |
| class, cell, or resource success guard fails | no performance-success disposition; a family kill requires the distinct mature, uncertainty-qualified rule in accepted `INV-00-06` |
| plausible-weight or adjacent-threshold sensitivity reverses the disposition | threshold-sensitive and ineligible to authorize planning |

A later accepted correctness defect must identify affected cells in a
defect-propagation record. All dependent cell, aggregate, resource, and
economic results are invalid until rerun or until independent review accepts
an argument that the defect cannot affect them.

## Hypothesis and falsifier

- Hypothesis: the proposed aggregate, subgroup, regression, resource, and
  confidence thresholds identify a broad user-visible improvement rather than
  noise, one favorable benchmark, one host, or disproportionate host-resource
  consumption.
- Falsifiers: Q-00-07 shows that 1.25× already clears or 1.50× fails the
  economic bar; plausible weights reverse the verdict; a valid workload metric
  cannot be converted to the oriented ratio; interval coverage fails under the
  observed hierarchy; host/SMP subgroup losses are masked; or a candidate can
  pass solely by spending unacceptable CPU, memory, or energy.
- Predicted outcomes: a robust pass clears all stated lower/upper bounds; a
  result that wins only on aggregate, only at 1 vCPU, or only under one weight
  set is not a performance success and remains inconclusive unless an accepted
  `INV-00-06` kill or extension rule applies.
