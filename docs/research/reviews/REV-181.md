## Implementation and validation findings

The historical I1–I14 bodies and T1–T4 calculations are unchanged. A separate
H-input validation rejects missing/extra histogram cells, noninteger/bool or
negative counts, denominator mismatch, duplicate arms and disagreement with
the recorded relaxed cell. Both histogram and timing output are suppressed
after an integrity or reporting-input failure. This is raw-schema checking,
not a newly asserted architectural invariant.

`Fraction` arithmetic classifies the symmetric max/min ratio exactly. Tests
include values immediately either side of 10/3 and 30, exact inclusive
endpoints, 3.33 versus 10/3, swapped fractions, either one-zero orientation
and both-zero undefined status. Corroboration requires a comparable pair,
positive matching plain exposure and zero strengthened exposure, after all
run checks; per-run exposures use exactly the comparison's run set. Fixtures
distinguish MP/UI zero from MP/background positive exposure, global I2 from
matching exposure, and pooled [0,2] exposure from a zero-exposure single run.

Pooling is explicit only: no discovery, directory scan or second positional
result. The envelope binds ordered paths, raw SHA-256 values, manifest IDs,
ten complete equal compatibility dimensions and a rationale to the canonical
cohort digest recorded in a digest-pinned independently accepted review.
Every member must be normal mode and pass integrity/H-input before any pooled
report. Duplicate identities, incompatible/missing context, review verdict or
independence failure, review/cohort/raw tampering and failed members suppress
the cohort. Compatibility exceptions are conservatively unsupported.

This byte/declaration validation is not reviewer authentication or proof that
context strings are factually supported. Real independent review must inspect
the supporting manifests, exact compatibility and approved producing method/
code. README explicitly rejects fabricated acceptance as research authority.
No existing review automatically becomes an exact cohort binding. The test
review records are labelled synthetic, are not entered into research ledgers,
and are removed with their temporary directories.

The weighted fixture deliberately uses plain denominators 1M/3M and
strengthened denominators 2M/1M: the correct pooled fractions are 2.8M/4M and
1.1M/3M, ratio 21/11, although averaging either pair's fractions yields .5.
Thus the test rejects both equal-weight fraction averaging and ratio
averaging. Cohort output has eight separated comparison rows and no pooled
timing thresholds.

Validation executed: `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3
experiments/INV-01-08/tests/histogram-fixtures.py` passed all 13 tests; these
also run the historical valid/negative/forbidden/negative-coverage checker
fixtures and assert suppression. `sh -n` on validate.sh and scoped

during the live timer experiment; the producer is unchanged. Both preserved

diagnostics and only MP/LDAR-STLR/background eligible; neither original
