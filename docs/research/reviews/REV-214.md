## Independent challenge and closure

### R1 — added lifetime and trace storage must actually enter the cap

The initial accounting sentence named up to2|H| setup epochs, two scratch
descriptors and original descriptor/request/trace counts. It did not clearly
add all |H| immutable setup descriptors and every new setup-call/transition
trace to the storage formula. Calling the formula conservative is insufficient
if extra lifetime-retained objects can be omitted. Requested an explicit
addition before the fixed capacity check, with no metadata recycling or
missing-output escape.

Closed by the author at the final subject: |H| immutable setup descriptors,
the two applicable scratch descriptors, the additional actual I/E/V replacement
descriptor, all added request epochs and every call/state trace are included.
Compiled sizeof/layout and original full counts remain inputs. Bounded
streaming can reuse trace-buffer storage only after checked output consumption,
not immutable descriptors/request identities; lost output fails. The16MiB
arena and256MiB owned metadata/trace cap remain unchanged; refusal is unresolved,
not permission to lower counts or recycle lifetime metadata. Actual native
allocation/serialization accounting still requires separate code200 inspection.

### R2 — conditional pressure release could be absent from a passing cell

The initial step5 only allowed release of H on an actual no-free attempt.
A legal counterexample repeatedly allocates the one free slot, replaces an
active generation and retires/releases the old active slot before the next
allocation. It maintains pressure occupancy and executes all ordinary floors
without ever releasing or reusing any H member. The scratch-only rejection
does not repair the missing genuine H release/publication witness and cannot
be called protected-reader backpressure. Requested an explicit acceptance
boundary rather than a combined generic pass label.

The author chose to require the genuine chain for every near-full I/E/V child.
Closed at the final subject: the second scratch reservation temporarily fills
the last free slot; an ordinary replacement allocation observes no eligible
slot. A genuinely retired H member is then released, reallocated with a fresh
token, published by the original arm and actually called by its intended
readers. Its removed active predecessor is retired using the original arm's
real condition, then retained. Finally scratch is discarded. The exact
retained set becomes old-set minus released-H plus retired-old-active-slot;
retained count is unchanged, two active entries per owner and one free slot
are restored before warmup. No fabricated ACK/hazard clear is admitted.

In V the released slot's recorded owner is explicitly the new builder and
executor, replacing that owner's page0 active entry. This preserves the
construction/execution identity boundary instead of introducing an unreviewed
cross-worker transfer. The additional genuine publication/descriptor and up
to two replacement epochs plus its traces are included in R1 accounting.

The cause of this finite no-free observation is recorded as retained slots
plus scratch-held capacity. Retired H members do not still have execution
rights. Protection-blocked retirement remains separately witnessed by the
original executable held-reader schedules. Neither setup nor scratch can
substitute for those schedules, and S/M do not inherit an I/E/V H-reuse claim.
This is a meaningful deliberately controlled pressure test, not an inference
that ordinary in-place patching requires a free code slot or a hard latency
bound. No required R2 correction remains.

Only then may the slot leave the free pool as retained retired code. Every
link and actual returned-pair witness is mandatory; a population count alone
cannot qualify the setup. Immutable descriptor lifetime continues through join.
