## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The evidentiary core of both runs is sound and verifies
  completely. All ten `MAN-01-01-01` digests and all five `MAN-01-10-01`
  digests recompute exactly, both `SHA256SUMS.txt` files verify with
  `shasum -c`, and every Observed number in both Results sections traces to a
  raw JSON field — the entitlement discriminator in both directions
  (`0xfae94007` / `0x00000000`), `max_vcpu_count` 64, the raw
  `0x000010000f100022` with no decoded field anywhere, the `42`/`43` JIT
  constants, aliasing, `46` for affinity on all three runs, and the whole
  `INV-01-10` inventory with its provenance labels. Every predeclared control
  ran and is retained, including a positive control that actually executed and
  distinguishes an entitlement effect from a signing defect, `codesign`
  captures for both arms, `kern.hv_support: 1` excluding the absent-hardware
  alternative, and negative controls failing with exactly their predicted
  single violations. The code-revision gap between the run stamp `274f479` and
  the reviewed `6c0acf5` is disclosed rather than hidden, and I confirmed the
  experiment directories are byte-identical between them. The relabelling is
  restrained in the way that matters: `KA-02`'s disposition is explicitly left
  unaffected by a successful run, and every disposition is bounded to this host
  and to ad-hoc signing. One defect blocks `accept`. `INV-01-10`'s environment
  capture omits the host identity entirely — `Mac16,10` occurs in none of its
  six files — so that artifact cannot support the applicability statement
  attached to it, and a later reviewer holding only the artifact could not tell
  which machine produced it. The fix is three added lines and a re-run.
- Applicability limitations: This review accepts nothing beyond the two named
  results, at revision `891fb03`. Both are valid for `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS
  26.6.2 (`25G83`) / SDK 26.4 / CLT `26.4.0.0.1774242506` and ad-hoc signing
  only; `P1` (M1 Pro / Sequoia 15) replication is owed before any platform-wide
  use, and Hardened-Runtime and `jit-write-allowlist` configurations are
  untested. `KA-01` and `KA-09` are "not falsified on this cell", not
  established platform-wide; `KA-02`/`F5` rest on documentary evidence this run
  does not touch. This review supplies no replication (`REV-095`) and no
  adversarial outcome (`REV-096`), and nothing here authorizes planning or
  implementation.
