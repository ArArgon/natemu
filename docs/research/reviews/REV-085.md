## Required changes

1. `INV-01-08` owner, return to result gate: bring the Provisional resolution
   into line with the run. Its "Not answered" paragraph still lists as
   "Hypothesis pending the evidence run" five things the same record now
   reports as Observed with numbers — the `CONSTRAINED UNPREDICTABLE` branch
   for atomics (`CLM-01-08-10`) and for non-atomic Load-Acquire
   (`CLM-01-08-19`), the fallback cost (`CLM-01-08-11`, 19.06 ns/op), the
   handshake cost (`CLM-01-08-13`), the `cas` retry-amplification separation
   (`CLM-01-08-18`, 6.0069 attempts per success at 8 threads), and the
   8-thread behaviour of both LSE arms. Rewrite it to state what the run
   answered, what it did not — chiefly that the SB comparison is inconclusive,
   so whether the host's public instructions close the ordering gap *in
   practice* genuinely remains unanswered — and keep `KA-06` undisposed, which
   it correctly is. While there, drop the closing governance sentence
   conditioning the record on an open source return review; `REV-084` closed
   it.
2. `INV-01-08` owner, return to result gate: state the positive control's
   margin per QoS arm, not only per run. The Results section reports "39 of
   10^6 at user-interactive and 1004 of 10^6 at background" and concludes "the
   harness does expose weak behaviour on this host". At background that is
   comfortable; at user-interactive it rests on 39 events, and this reviewer's
   replication of the same code on the same host recorded **1** event in 10^6
   in that arm (`MAN-01-08-03`). Record that the user-interactive positive
   control is marginal, that `I2`'s any-arm rule is what kept both runs alive,
   and that a user-interactive SB result therefore carries much less power
   than a background one. Do not weaken `CLM-01-08-08`, which is true as
   written for this run; add the margin as a limitation.
3. `INV-01-08` owner, return to result gate: cite or withdraw the "±30–50%
   run-to-run variance band reported on this host". It is used twice — once in
   the Results preamble and once to argue the `T4b` miss "should not be read
   as a stable 67×" — and it carries weight, because it is the record's only
   mitigation of a predeclared threshold miss. It cites no manifest, review,
   or record. Either name the artifact that establishes the band and state
   which metrics it covers, or remove the softening and let the miss stand on
   its own. This reviewer's replication measured 66.27× against 67.40×, so on
   the available evidence the `T4b` ratio is reproducible to within 2% and the
   softening is unsupported for this metric.

## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: the run itself is sound and the reporting is, in the main,
  unusually honest. All eight digests across the two runs recompute and match
  both manifests and both `sha256sums.txt` files; the environment capture
  matches the stated host in every field and records the `FEAT_LSE2` state the
  atomics claims are conditional on; the producing revision is the accepted
  one, verified independently by extracting `d8cb40c` and hashing it rather
  than by reading the manifest; the negative control failed exactly `I2` and
  `I3` on real data; and every Observed number in the record traces to the raw
  `result.json` exactly, including all twelve histograms and all six LSE arms.
  Most creditably, the frozen histogram adjudication was applied against the
  author's own interest and the record reports its headline result as a
  negative. `accept` is prohibited by three reporting defects, none of which
  touches a measurement: a Provisional resolution left stale enough to
  contradict the Results section above it, an unstated positive-control margin
  that this reviewer's replication shows can fall to a single event, and an
  uncited variance claim used to soften the one threshold miss — a claim the
  replication contradicts for that metric.
- Applicability limitations: every Observed figure is valid for `Mac16,10`
  (M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (25G83) / SDK 26.4 only, on a shared host with other
  agents active. Nothing here disposes `KA-06`, establishes that the
  architecture forbids any outcome, or transfers to `P1` (M1 Pro / Sequoia
  15), where the Arm feature level differs and replication remains owed. This
  review approves no synthesis use, no planning, and no implementation, and it
  does not review `INV-01-07`, whose run failed integrity rule `I16` and
  correctly produced no claim.
