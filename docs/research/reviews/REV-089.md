## Verdict

- Verdict: `accept`
- Rationale: All three required actions are verifiably closed, and the one that
  mattered most is closed at the root. The indirect-branch target now has a slot
  no helper can write, which is the property the previous fix lost when it made
  the target share `cpu->tmp` with the store helper; a whole-file grep confirms
  the slot has exactly one writer and one reader, and a guest indirect call plus
  invariant `I16` mean the path is now exercised rather than silent. I did not
  accept that `I16` works: I reverted the fix in a scratch build and the run
  completed with `abort: null`, `icall_marker = 0x0`, and `I16` as the only
  failing invariant — so the defect that previously produced a PASS now produces
  a specific, targeted FAIL. The two plumbing fixes are equally real: the
  synthesized abort document yields `I1a` instead of a `KeyError`, with the
  short circuit narrow enough that `--inject-skip-restart` still witnesses its
  seven targeted invariants, and the unwitnessed path now carries exit 2 through

  deliberately mis-targeted control. Nothing regressed under re-execution: five

  plus the synthetic assertion, ThreadSanitizer reports zero races, `run.sh`
