## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: Integrity is sound and most citation work is of the standard
  `REV-032` set. All seven cited SDK headers reproduce their ledger SHA-256
  exactly, all three Apple content renditions reproduce theirs, every cited line
  range resolves to the text the record quotes, and every availability claim in
  the subject — `pthread_jit_write_protect_np` and
  `pthread_jit_write_protect_supported_np` at `macos(11.0)`,
  `pthread_jit_write_with_callback_np` at `macos(11.4)`,
  `pthread_jit_write_freeze_callbacks_np` at `macos(12.1)`, and
  `hv_vm_create`/`hv_vm_get_max_vcpu_count`/`hv_vcpu_create`/`hv_vcpu_get_sys_reg`
  at `macos(11.0)` — is literally present as an availability tag at the named
  lines, with the `hv_vm_create(_:)` rendition's own `introducedAt: "11.0"`
  metadata agreeing. No availability claim is asserted from a capture that does
  not state it, no secondary source carries a load-bearing claim, and no
  Inferred claim is dressed as Source-backed — the record is unusually careful
  in labelling `CLM-01-01-11`'s decode and `CLM-01-01-15`'s rule as Inferred.
  `accept` is nonetheless prohibited. Two claims are contradicted by the very
  sections they cite: `CLM-01-01-07` miscounts a ten-enumerator enum as five
  (S1), and `CLM-01-01-09` reads a parameter-scoped "Apple silicon only."
  annotation as a function-scoped one on a page whose Discussion documents the
  Intel signature (S2). One source-to-claim mapping points at a claim its
  section cannot support while the claim it does support has no source row (S3).
  The `ID_AA64MMFR0_EL1` field layout that the probe applies and a claim names
  has no source in the inventory and no support anywhere in a 91-file capture
  store, on a track that already cites the Arm ARM eight times (S4). And the
  arm64-only premise on which `KA-02` and family `F5` turn is carried by no
  claim at all, although the sentences that would settle it are already captured
  and digest-matched (S5). Each is concrete and checkable, so `revise` with the
  eight numbered changes above.
