## Required changes

1. `INV-01-07` owner, return to method gate: make `T3` the same quantity in
   the record and in the frozen checker. The Metrics table defines it as
   round-trip p99 < 200 µs, derived as one fifth of the 1 ms tick;
   `CLM-01-07-15` states the round-trip form and calls half of it the one-way
   upper bound; the Metrics "Delivery latency (one-way)" row says explicitly
   that the one-way figure "is **not** a standalone threshold, because it
   subtracts two threads' timestamps and no source in this inventory states
   that the clock is a system-wide comparable time base". `check.py`
   nevertheless computes `T3` as one-way `p99_ns < 100000` and prints the
   derivation "one tenth of the 1 ms guest tick period". The two are not
   interchangeable: rule `I17` only requires one-way ≤ round trip, so a run
   with a one-way p99 of 90 µs and a round-trip p99 of 250 µs would read MEET
   in the checker and MISS against the record. This is the identical defect
   `REV-044` change 14 closed for `T4`, reintroduced for `T3` by the same
   return, and the record's own rule — thresholds are fixed in `check.py` and
   may not change once data exists — makes fixing it before any run
   mandatory.
2. `INV-01-08` owner, return to method gate: say how the outcome histogram
   adjudicates alternatives (a) and (b), and predeclare the reading. The
   histogram was added because `BP-01-08-start` holds that "a bare 'not
   observed' is uninterpretable", but the Alternative explanations section is
   unchanged on that point and still calls (a) versus (b) "distinguished only
   weakly" while naming only the plain arm as the control. The histogram does
   better than that, and it also shows the concern is not hypothetical: in the
   reviewer's reduced-count run the user-interactive SB arms recorded the
   both-sides-observed outcome `r0!=0,r1!=0` in 0 of 50 000 iterations under
   plain `LDR`/`STR` and in 49 979 of 50 000 under `DMB ISH`, so the two arms
   sampled almost disjoint interleaving distributions. State that a
   strengthened arm whose histogram differs sharply from its plain
   counterpart's is reported as an inconclusive comparison rather than as
   corroboration of `CLM-01-08-02`, and name the comparison the result gate
   will apply. (Reviewer run, `--iters 50000` under concurrent load: not
   evidence, no manifest, supports no claim.)

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: thirteen of the fifteen `REV-044` required changes are closed and
  were re-derived here by executing the probes rather than by reading the gate
  table — the cross-thread comparability assumption is now bounded by a

  used in the section that needs it — where the reviewer's own run shows the
  strengthened and plain arms sampling almost disjoint interleavings. The
  method gate also cannot close ahead of `REV-063`.
