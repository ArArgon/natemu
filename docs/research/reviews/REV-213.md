## Discovery and proposed resolution

The original pressure bullet does not specify how to distinguish a real
PUBLISHED→RETIRED lifetime observation from constructed but never executed
bytes, or how fixed-slot S/M patching should exercise allocator exhaustion.
The draft setup cannot be called a passing retirement/backpressure experiment.
This is a needed method clarification, not a forced-positive host finding.

M18-P1 requires every held pressure generation to be published, actually called
by the intended reader set, removed through coordinator ownership, drained via
the exact epoch/hazard rule, and only then retired and retained. The setup
prefix is explicitly separated from stress and does not enter cost samples.
The per-arm transition uses the existing I/S/E/V lifetime rules; M's prefix
does not count as a mapping observation. Every pressure slot has an auditable
causal chain. V ownership remains construction and execution on that worker.

An additional allocator-only last-free-slot reservation/rejection/discard is
proposed for near-full cells. Scratch allocations acquire no execution right;
they cannot satisfy retirement, protected-reader completion, or executable
reuse criteria. S/M retain genuine in-place reuse and quiescence checks on
their active slots, while the allocator component observation is separately
labeled. I/E/V retain actual allocation pressure and safe release-before-reuse
of genuinely retired pressure slots. There is no combined label silently
equating these observations or claiming a hard completion bound.

The supplement retains every full stress count, 1/2/4/8 and local signing
dimension, block-size/fragmentation cell and immutable descriptor. It adds
exact setup-generation and bounded setup-epoch accounting before the same
16 MiB / 256 MiB cap check; refusal remains unresolved rather than changing
batch size or recycling metadata. Existing source claims, cost prerequisite,
platform debts and architectural boundaries are unchanged.

Pre-verdict clarification for REV-214: each near-full I/E/V child now requires
an actual no-free→retired-H-release→new-token-publication/call chain. The
second scratch reservation is explicitly the cause of exhaustion; it supplies
no execution-protected-reader claim. V retains the released slot's owner as
builder/executor. The removed active generation is retired by its original
arm before becoming retained, and the swapped retained/free sets are recorded.
The cap formula explicitly adds all H descriptors, two applicable scratch
descriptors, one additional I/E/V replacement descriptor/publication, up to
2H+2 epochs, and all added call/transition buffer storage. Existing actual
held-reader hooks remain independent mandatory observations. These authored
clarifications still await the independent verdict.
