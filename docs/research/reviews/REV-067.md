## Verdict

- Verdict: `accept`
- Rationale: The return fixes the structural defect rather than papering over
  it. `KA-02` is no longer decided by an arm64 register read: the record states
  in its own words why that read tested a different proposition, why no probe on
  this platform could test the real one, and then decides the assumption
  documentarily — `KA-02` false, family `F5` killed under `INV-00-06` family-kill
  item 1, applicable to every accepted Apple Silicon cell without `P1`
  replication because the SDK architecture gate is not host- or
  OS-version-specific. The register read survives, correctly relabelled as an
  `F3` input in the claims, the metrics, and the falsifier section, with an
  explicit statement that the disposition is neither corroborated nor weakened
  by it. The remaining nine changes are closed and were re-derived rather than
  taken on report: the two documented JIT configurations are now named, the
  callback metric demoted to corroborating evidence in the untested
  configuration, and the Hardened-Runtime/Developer-ID gap stated as a coverage
  limit routed to `Q-01-04`; `kern.hv_support` is read, reported with a
  readability flag, captured in the environment, and wired into the exclusion
  criteria and the alternative-explanation set; the positive control is an
  executed comparison writing its own artifact instead of a sentence; the
  affinity result has a claim of its own; the two-sample clock reading is
  labelled a smoke check that establishes nothing; and all four metric defects
  are fixed, including the checker outcome, which I verified by injection now
  fails on a run where `KA-01` is falsified. The admissibility rule that
  licensed the circular treatment of a foreign architecture's register layout
  gained an exclusion, so the fix is at the rule rather than at the one claim.
  No required action remains open, every applicable dimension carries a
  disposition, and independence is met, so `accept` is permitted.
- Applicability limitations: This review accepts the method gate for
  `INV-01-01` and nothing else, at revision `6c0acf5`. It establishes no
  platform capability, no `KA-01` disposition, and no Observed result: every
  measured claim remains a Hypothesis and no evidence run has occurred. The
  `KA-02`/`F5` disposition it finds methodologically sound is documentary and
  still requires the adversarial gate before entering candidate synthesis. Probe
  values quoted above come from reviewer smoke and fault-injection runs in a
  scratch directory, are not evidence, and support no claim. This review does not
  accept the sources (`REV-066`) or the code (`REV-068`), and nothing here
  authorizes an evidence run, planning, or implementation.
