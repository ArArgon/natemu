## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: both methods are recognisably rigorous — predeclared negative
  controls whose injected defect this reviewer confirmed the checkers catch,
  correctly justified single-run sample plans for boolean capability checks,
  explicit no-post-hoc-exclusion rules, honest single-host applicability
  matrices, named confounders including the one that matters most
  (`DISPATCH_SOURCE_TYPE_MEMORYPRESSURE` is host-wide and cannot attribute
  pressure to an instance), and a genuinely proposed rule for the `BP-00-end`
  tension rather than a restatement of it. `accept` is nevertheless prohibited.
  Twelve required actions are open, and three of them go to whether the
  experiments can answer their own questions: `INV-01-09` cannot attribute a
  termination of the entitled arm to the restricted entitlement because it runs
  no benign-entitlement control, and it holds two incompatible correctness
  rules for the one metric whose disagreement case a scratch run already
  produced; `INV-01-14` cannot interpret the `setrlimit` `EINVAL` that a
  scratch run already produced, because no prior or read-back limit is in its
  schema. Beyond those, a breadth-pass requirement to record the executing UID
  was dropped rather than met, the descriptor-enforcement metric is declared
  but not applied, the CPU row's arithmetic is not carried to the two-peer case
  the containment oracle requires, the backstop formula has an undefined free
  variable, the thread-summation premise motivating the CPU design is never
  tested, half of the `BP-00-end` question is unanswered, and half of
  `INV-01-14`'s stated method — the limit-proposal derivation — has no
  specified procedure and produces no numbers in four of seven rows.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict covers the proposed methods of
  `INV-01-09` and `INV-01-14` only, at `ba66757` and `5fe6a68` respectively,
  and expressly records `INV-01-13`'s method gate as `not-applicable` rather
  than passing or failing it. It approves no source claim, no experiment code,
  no raw result, no replication, no adversarial finding, and no synthesis
  outcome; it establishes no macOS behavior, no device-backing or
  resource-budget finding, and no `KA-08`/`KA-12`/`KA-13` disposition; and it
  authorizes no evidence run, no planning, and no implementation. The scratch
  executions cited above were smoke observations made to test the method's
  own claims about itself; they produced no artifact under the raw root and
  support no claim.
