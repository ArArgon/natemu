## Independent closure findings

R1 is closed. One operative Controls/Confounders/Metrics/Sample plan set now
governs. Retained immutable descriptor inspection is allowed after code
retirement; code execution/retained execution rights are not. SUPERSEDED is
not RETIRED, and an already protected old call may finish. Those distinctions
agree across the method, hook table, metrics and code-validation contract.

R2 is closed at the method gate. E/V now require SC entries/page versions,
immutable request records, release/acquire exact request and acknowledgement
mailboxes, and post-ACK SC reload plus the acquired version floor. Consider
entry replacement followed by request publication: a reader acquiring that
request drops its old pointer before ACK and cannot resume through the old
local pointer. Coordinator retirement follows every matching ACK; one
outstanding epoch prevents a later request from silently replacing the record
being acknowledged. A zero entry waits. V must not withhold an ACK while
waiting for a coordinator installation, avoiding that stated drain deadlock.

The previous final-check/install counterexample is excluded by a concrete
ownership rule: either check+installation occurs first and the subsequent
invalidation removes and drains it, or invalidation completes first and the
new version check rejects the stale candidate. Invalidation holds the same
ownership through its ACK drain, and no installation/version mutation can
interleave. The new successful-check/pre-install hook tests precisely that
gap and requires the attempted invalidator to queue. The inverse schedule
and deliberately unowned delayed installation remain required negatives.
I's version boundary retains its separate SC-hazard safety condition; S/M
retain matching STOP/RESUME exclusion. These are abstract protocol arguments,
not proofs of instruction visibility or executable correctness.

R3 is closed. The per-arm table admits the previous counterexample as valid:
an I/E writer held before publication does not prevent a reader from calling
the current old entry. Pre-advertisement, advertised-hazard, protected-call,
post-publication and post-ACK states have distinct obligations. E/V may
progress after ACK on a floor-valid entry; S/M alone require RESUME.
Coordinator sequence numbers and arrive/attempt/release handshakes provide
causal observations; hooks cannot clear hazards, supply ACKs, grant mutation
ownership or introduce a global reader lock. Both the independent finite
model and executable hooks at actual reader topologies must admit these
allowed paths and reject violations; unhooked stress remains mandatory.

R4 is closed. Zero clock pairs remain in the full distributions; all-zero
coordinator calibration is unresolved, not a backward clock. The checked
Fpre/Ffinal formulas, four candidate sizes and 64 calibration batches per
size are fixed. Every candidate batch must exceed Fpre, the smallest eligible
size is frozen before evidence, and all 1000 evidence batches and reported
phases must exceed Ffinal. Otherwise the entire child's quantitative result
is unresolved with raw data retained; no favorable positive-duration subset
or post-evidence retuning is permitted. Backward readings/overflow are
separate invalidity conditions. Same-coordinator causal intervals, nested
sample labels and no pure-API subtraction remain explicit.
