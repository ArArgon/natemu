- R1: preserve Linux's modal language (`typically`, `ideally`, and “ideal and
  customary”) and distinguish clocksource, clock events, and `sched_clock`.
- R2: identify `SRC-160` as a historical, KVM/hardware-virtualization document
  carried in the v6.12 tree; use it only for a risk and alternatives taxonomy,
  never as current distribution behavior or evidence about NatEmu.
- R3: keep every Apple clock/timer claim host-side and the Intel clauses at the
  architectural-mechanism boundary; infer no guest policy or latency bound.
- R4: make the unresolved exact CPUID/MSR/clocksource contract, P1/P2 evidence,
  lifecycle dependencies, and 2/4/8-vCPU analysis explicit before any policy
  can be admitted or any in-guest benchmark can be accepted.

All changes are present in the packet. An independent reviewer must reproduce
the captures and extractions and challenge each cross-source/absence inference.
