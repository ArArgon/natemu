## Required changes

1. `INV-01-07` owner, return to method gate: establish or bound cross-thread
   clock comparability before any cross-thread latency figure is interpreted.
   `CLM-01-07-15` and the whole `signal` arm subtract a timestamp taken by the
   target thread inside a signal handler or after a wait from a timestamp
   taken by the sender thread. No claim in the record, and no source in its
   inventory, states that `mach_absolute_time` or `CLOCK_UPTIME_RAW` is a
   single system-wide time base comparable across threads or cores; `SRC-096`
   says only that the clock "increments monotonically in tick units (starting
   at an arbitrary point)", and `SRC-097` says of `CLOCK_MONOTONIC_RAW` that
   "It should not be compared to other system time sources". The record itself
   concedes under Open correctness risks that the `cross_thread_clock` arm
   "serializes on a mutex, so it establishes ordering under contention rather
   than free-running comparability". Choose one: add a Source-backed claim
   with a primary citation for a system-wide time base; or add an arm that
   bounds cross-thread skew independently — the `INV-01-08` `roundtrip` arm
   already measures a full handshake on a single thread's clock and is the
   obvious model; or restate `CLM-01-07-15` as a round-trip figure with the
   one-way latency reported only as an upper bound of half the round trip.
   The record must not carry a one-way cross-thread p99 that rests on an
   unstated assumption.
2. Both owners, return to method gate: reconcile the integrity-rule
   identifiers with the frozen checkers. `INV-01-07`'s Correctness status
   names `I1`–`I10` where `check.py` has `I0`–`I12`; `INV-01-08`'s Correctness
   status and Negative controls name `I1`–`I10` and say `--negative` fails
   "`I1` and `I2`" where the checker has `I1`–`I14` and the reviewer's
   `--negative` run failed `I2` (positive control) and `I3` (concurrency).
   `experiments/INV-01-08/README.md` carries the same stale numbering. A
   correctness criterion that names the wrong rule cannot be checked.
3. `INV-01-07` owner, return to method gate: either add a
   `DISPATCH_TIMER_STRICT` arm or narrow the dispatch claim. The arm is
   labelled `dispatch_source_timer_leeway0` and `CLM-01-07-13` calls it "a
   zero-leeway `dispatch_source` timer", but the cited man page states that
   with leeway 0 "the lower limit is under the control of the system" and that
   `DISPATCH_TIMER_STRICT` is the documented way to ask the system to observe
   a smaller leeway. As written the arm measures the coalesced default; a `T2`
   miss attributed to dispatch would be confounded with an unexercised
   documented mitigation.
4. `INV-01-08` owner, return to method gate: make the `cas` arm comparable to
   the `ldadd` arm, or stop comparing them. The `cas` worker executes a
   relaxed load, a `cas`, and a retry until success, and the reported
   `ns_per_op` divides elapsed time by *successful* operations, so failed
   attempts under contention are charged to the atomic's per-operation cost.
   The reviewer's run gave `ldadd` 2.70 → 13.88 ns/op from 2 to 8 threads
   (5.1×, `T4` MEET) but `cas` 3.62 → 198.10 ns/op (54.7×, `T4` MISS), and the
   difference is dominated by retry amplification rather than by the
   instruction's scaling. `CLM-01-08-12` asserts a single "within 20×" bound
   over both as if they were the same measurement. Either count and report
   attempts separately from successes, or split `CLM-01-08-12` into an
   instruction claim and a CAS-loop claim, each with its own predeclared
   threshold and its own derivation.
5. `INV-01-08` owner, return to method gate: satisfy the two recording
   requirements `BP-01-08-start` sets under Measurement failure modes. Record
   the full outcome histogram for each litmus arm — all four register-value
   combinations, not only the count matching the relaxed outcome — because the
   breadth pass states that "a bare 'not observed' is uninterpretable" and the
   current record emits only `relaxed_observed` and `iterations`. Then either
   implement thread start-offset randomization or record its absence as a
   predeclared limitation naming the interleavings the fixed three-phase
   barrier makes unreachable.
6. `INV-01-08` owner, return to method gate: justify or reclassify the 10^6
   iteration floor. The sample plan at `5fe6a68` correctly withdrew the claim

## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: both methods are unusually careful in the places that most often
  go wrong — the instrument is characterized before it is used, cost is
  explicitly excluded from both falsifiers, non-observation is refused as
  proof of prohibition, exclusions are genuinely empty, placement is treated
  as sampled rather than controlled, and `INV-01-08` makes SMP a baseline
  rather than an extension. The sample arithmetic is correct as stated. But
  `accept` is prohibited: the cross-thread delivery measurement rests on an
  unstated and untested clock-comparability assumption; the records name
  integrity rules that no longer exist; a predeclared metric and its frozen
  implementation disagree; the `cas` arm is not the measurement its claim
  describes; two recording requirements the record's own breadth pass sets are
  unmet; the multicore wakeup rate the guest requirement names is not covered;
  four decision-bearing thresholds have no derivation; and the `T2` condition
  is simultaneously a threshold and a falsifier with two stated consequences,
  on a host where this reviewer measured it missing by about 2×. The method
  gate also cannot close ahead of `REV-043` required change 1, because the
  controls were calibrated against a source statement that review returns.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict covers the methods of `INV-01-07` at
  `c497cf3` and `INV-01-08` at `5fe6a68` only. It approves no source claim, no
  experiment code, no raw result, and no replication, and it selects no
  synthesis outcome. Every number quoted from the reviewer's own reduced-count
  runs is a review-validation observation on `Mac16,10` / macOS 26.6.2
  (25G83) / SDK 26.4 under concurrent load; it is not evidence, it is not
  under the raw root, no manifest exists for it, and it supports no claim in
  any record.
