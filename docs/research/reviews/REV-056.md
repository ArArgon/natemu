## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: Ten of `REV-041`'s eleven required changes are closed, several by a
  better route than the action demanded. The bitmap differential — the item this
  reviewer was asked to decide and decided against the record — was not
  disclaimed away but redesigned: the whole-loop pair is retained, explicitly
  marked uninterpretable as a bitmap cost and re-filed as its own claim about
  the method, while a new isolated pair removes the cache-missing store from
  both sides and now resolves a real marginal, +0.0859 ns/op with the sign
  consistent across all eight repetitions against a 0.0179 ns/op criterion built
  from both variants' spreads, with the emulator-relevant figure still routed to
  `Q-04-01`. The two controls that could not fail now fail: re-injecting the
  exact defects from `REV-041` and `REV-042`, a thread A that never acquires
  write access aborts the `INV-01-04` probe instead of yielding an identical
  block, and a handler that misattributes every fault draws two explicit
  `FAIL`s from `INV-01-05`. The precision target is met at 513 ticks against a
  target of 100 and missing it is now a stop condition; the timebase is emitted
  by all four probes; the page-size reading count is corrected and enforced; the
  `mincore` candidate is reopened with a genuinely informative probe;
  async-signal-safety is declared and routed; and the 16 KiB premise is bound to
  its single-host Hypothesis in terms. `accept` is prohibited on one residual:
  `INV-01-06`'s claim inventory, discovery entry, and Baselines bullet still
  describe two resume paths that the returned probe does not have, so
  `CLM-01-06-08` now cites an artifact field that is not emitted and predicts a
  comparison the code cannot perform. The fix reached the code, the Metrics
  table, and the README but not the claim. One required change is open.
