## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The replication succeeded on everything that decides feasibility
  and failed to reproduce every number that describes cost. Executing the
  approved code at its recorded revision `cee40cb` — extracted with
  `git archive`, not taken from the working tree — produced a `probe` binary
  byte-identical to the original in all four investigations, and for
  `INV-01-03` a byte-identical `result.json` as well. Checked by this
  reviewer's own `verify.py`, written against the records' declared thresholds
  rather than against the author's controls and containing eleven distinct ways
  to fail, all four runs read `PASS`, with the author's checker independently
  reading `PASS` on the normal output and `FAIL` on the negative output in
  every case. Every capability, soundness, attribution, granularity, and
  signal-class result reproduced exactly: 2000/2000 tracked faults with every
  address named, 1000 identity matches with zero misattribution, `EINVAL` on
  the sub-host-page `mprotect` with no silent rounding, three distinct fault
  signals each resumed to the declared sentinel with `uc_mcsize` matching, zero
  missed faults over 3000, both concurrent threads seeing their own state, and
  the cross-thread witness held. Against that, no timing magnitude reproduced
  to the quoted precision: −52.9% on delivery latency, −27.1% on toggle cost,
  +40.0% on `mprotect`, +631% on the round-trip maximum, and a factor of 4.2 on
  the concurrent-cost effect the record already flagged as a partial — at
  comparable host load, 1.90–2.39 here against 2.43–2.47 there, so the recorded
  load does not explain it. The one robust exception is the isolated bitmap
  marginal, +11.5% and sign-consistent in both runs, which vindicates the
  redesign required at `REV-041` action 4. Because the records state these
  magnitudes as Observed numbers, `accept` would certify a precision the
  evidence does not support; `revise` is the honest verdict, and the two
  required changes ask for a limitation rather than for different results. The
  claimed results reproduced in kind; their magnitudes did not.
- Applicability limitations: This replication was executed on the same host as
  the original runs and therefore **widens nothing**: applicability remains
  `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (`25G83`) / SDK 26.4 / Apple clang 21.0.0 /
  ad-hoc signing with no hardened runtime, at 1 and 2 threads with uncontrolled
  placement. `P1` (M1 Pro / Sequoia 15) replication remains owed before any
  platform-wide use, as do the Developer ID / hardened-runtime cell, the
  ptrauth-enabled build cell, and 4- and 8-vCPU scaling. This review decides the
  replication gate only; it supplies no result acceptance (`REV-075`), no
  adversarial conclusion (`REV-077`), and no synthesis outcome, and nothing here
  authorizes planning or implementation.
