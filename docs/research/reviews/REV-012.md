  scope contract rather than evidence of a target population; preserve the
  `Q-06-02` dependency before adoption, weighting, sponsorship, or economic use.
- `INV-00-01` owner, return to adversarial gate: make every workload concrete
  enough to reproduce (revisions/digests, package/test selections, config,
  cache/reset/client placement, timeout, success/failure/skip oracle), and
  require accepted Track 02 correctness for the measured cell.
- `INV-00-01` owner, return to adversarial gate: divide mandatory and
  conditional workloads, give conditional U3/U4 work zero success/economic
  weight until full-system necessity passes, and define 2/4/8-vCPU, placement,
  topology, and contention coverage or an explicit limitation.
- `INV-00-07` owner, return to adversarial gate: publish a reviewed input and
  accounting specification for `PVC`/`PVB`, including non-overlapping labor,
  schedule, compute, admin, hardware, and shared-CI allocations; separate
  adoption/reliability and define input provenance/exclusions.
- `INV-00-07` owner, return to adversarial gate: define valuation dates,
  discounted payback, capital/residual-hardware treatment, portfolio/conditional
  weighting, joint correlated downside construction, and sensitivity/ownership
  for the 10%/BCR/payback policy values.
- `INV-00-07` owner, return to adversarial gate: make every sponsorship route
  auditable and binding enough for its claimed volume/funding, with affiliated
  organization definition, duplicate-benefit prevention, concentration and
  withdrawal downside tests, five-year maintenance funding, and zero credit for
  uncommitted sponsorship.
- Both owners, return to the applicable source and adversarial gates: close
  `REV-008`; do not admit these resolutions to candidate synthesis until the
  listed Track 02, Track 05, and `Q-06-02`/`Q-06-03` dependencies are accepted.

## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The records usefully precommit to a diverse full-system direction
  and reject naive wall-clock-to-labor conversion, but representative-user
  evidence, workload concreteness/oracles, conditional-user handling,
  topology/platform coverage, and the economic accounting/downside/sponsorship
  rules still admit material counterexamples and double counting. Required
  changes are unresolved, so an independent adversarial acceptance is barred.
- Applicability limitations: This review establishes no demand, adoption,
  full-system necessity, correctness, performance, price realization, NPV,
  sponsorship, platform-wide value, synthesis outcome, planning authorization,
  or implementation authorization.
