## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: This is a strong method and the breadth pass behind it is the best
  in the track — it anticipated composition-versus-coexistence, oracle
  blindness, control determinism, coalescing, instrument perturbation, and
  post-hoc oracle adjustment, and the method discharges most of what it set.
  The sample plan, the exclusion of wall time from every aggregate, the
  method-first sequencing with no evidence run and no manifest, the refusal to
  say "TSO is preserved", the explicit-check narrowing of `KA-04`, the
  `F2`-instance-not-family boundary with `F3` screened in neither direction,
  and the `INV-00-02` confound are all correct and survive independent reading.
  Eleven required changes nonetheless block `accept`, and three are
  load-bearing rather than editorial. The concurrency oracle does not support
  the word "simultaneously" that `CLM-01-12-09` uses, and its overlap threshold
  is satisfied by both threads merely being alive — a point the record's own
  breadth pass raised and the method did not answer. The predeclared
  configuration is not enforced by the predeclared oracle: I falsified
  `jit_mapping`, `loop_k`, `loop_n`, and `injection` on a real positive
  document and the checker PASSed each time, so a run in which `MAP_JIT` failed
  or in which the contended loop ran once would be indistinguishable from the
  frozen experiment. And the unwitnessed-invariant list — the record's own
  defence against oracle blindness — is inaccurate in both directions. Two
  breadth-pass obligations (A/D observables, fault-versus-interrupt priority)
  were dropped silently, one declared stop criterion has no implementation, and
  one deliberate-simplification argument is contradicted by the code it
  defends. `accept` is prohibited while those actions are open.
- Applicability limitations: This review judges the `INV-01-12` method and its
  predeclared oracles only. It accepts no source mapping, no experiment code,
  no result, and no `KA-04`, `KA-06`, or `KA-11` disposition; no evidence run
  exists and none may be performed until the method and code gates pass. Every
  number quoted here is from reviewer scratch runs on `Mac16,10` (M4) /
  macOS 26.6.2 (25G83) / SDK 26.4, is diagnostic of the oracle's
  discrimination only, supports no claim in any record, and states nothing
  about `P1` or about any platform-wide behaviour. Nothing here authorizes
  planning or implementation.
