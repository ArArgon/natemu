## Required changes

1. `INV-01-07` owner, return to method gate: cite or restate the empirical
   basis for the enforcement narrowing. The record justifies restricting
   `I16` to the user-interactive arms with an uncited measurement —
   "background arms still overshoot by tens of microseconds at the larger
   targets while the matched user-interactive arms sit at hundreds of
   nanoseconds" — and this reviewer's corrected-bracket run at `--n 60` does
   not reproduce it: every background same-thread arm sat inside tolerance
   (875, 2 166, 9 583 ns for `mach_wait_until` at 100 µs / 1 ms / 10 ms; 918,
   1 000, 1 500 ns for `nanosleep`), and the user-interactive 10 ms arm was
   2 084 ns, not "hundreds of nanoseconds". Either name the smoke artifact with
   its sample count and per-arm values so the gate can check the claim, or drop
   the quantitative sentence and rest the narrowing on the argument that
   actually carries it — that ε is a property of the timebase ratio and appears
   identically at any QoS, so enforcing on the protected arms loses no
   detection power, while the background arms' paired-read windows are
   unprotected against deschedule. The second form is sound as it stands and I
   verified it by injection; the first form is a quantitative claim the method
   gate cannot check.
2. `INV-01-07` owner, return to method gate: state the tolerance's effective
   sensitivity per target rather than only its formula. Because the 2 µs floor
   dominates at the smaller targets, `2 µs + 0.1% of target` detects a ratio
   error of roughly ε > 1.4% at 100 µs, ε > 0.3% at 1 ms, and ε > 0.08% at
   10 ms — not 0.1% throughout, which is what "the 0.1% term is what detects a
   conversion artefact" currently implies in both the record and the
   experiment README. Add the three figures and the observation that they are
   ample for the failure mode the control targets, since a real
   `mach_timebase_info` misuse on this host would be a factor of 41.67 rather
   than a fraction of a percent. This changes no number in the rule.

## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: the correction is substantively right and is the fix a method
  gate should want. I re-derived both bracket forms rather than accepting the
  record's account, and the old form provably reduces to `gap + tail − E·ε`
  with an unbounded deschedule inside `gap` — the defect the failed run
  exposed — while the new form reduces to `(tail′ − gap′) − E·ε` with only two
  adjacent clock reads in the windows and the `target_ns` term cancelled out.
  The tolerance is derived from the quantity's own error sources and is
  *tighter* at the target where the failure occurred, which is the opposite of
  a post-hoc rescue. Most importantly, the narrowing is not what rescues the
  failed run: my corrected-bracket run puts that exact arm at 875 ns against a
  2 100 ns tolerance, so the bracket fix alone would have cleared it even with
  background still enforced. The failed run is retained untouched, with its
  digests intact and its diagnosis explicitly not promoted to a conclusion.
  `accept` is nevertheless prohibited by two documentation defects in the
  material the method gate is being asked to approve: the narrowing's stated
  empirical basis is uncited and I could not reproduce it, and the tolerance's
  advertised sensitivity is not what the rule delivers at two of the three
  targets. Neither is a measurement defect and neither endangers a re-run.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict covers the `INV-01-07` method at
  `13c6e98` only. It approves no result, replication, or synthesis use, and it
  does not itself authorize a run. Every number quoted from the reviewer's
  execution is a review-validation observation on `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS
  26.6.2 (25G83) / SDK 26.4 at `--n 60` under concurrent load; it is not
  evidence, is not under the raw root, has no manifest, and no record may cite
  it. In particular, a worst-case-over-N metric grows with N, so nothing in my
  reduced-count run bounds what a 2000-sample run will show — a point the
  record's own disclosure already makes.
