## Verdict

- Verdict: `accept`
- Rationale: both required changes are closed, and both were closed by
  strengthening the record rather than by softening the finding. The
  positive-control gap is stated three times, at the claim, in the alternatives,
  and in the limitations, each time saying the same thing — that the run shows
  the call did not succeed, not why — and the correction is careful enough to
  keep what the evidence *does* establish (a degraded-but-working mode is
  excluded) separate from what it does not (the cause). The L2 finding, which
  was the more consequential of the two, is closed at both ends: `INV-01-09`
  now says `KA-08`'s network clause survives only through the disjunct that
  cannot move Ethernet frames and names the consequence for `Q-01-13` and the
  licence routing to `Q-01-11`, and `INV-01-13` carries it as an Open cell
  instead of inheriting an unqualified premise — a cross-reference this
  reviewer verified rather than assumed, along with the `BP-01-09-start`
  attribution the amended text rests on. Re-reading the full record set for
  collateral damage found none: no claim was relabelled, no confidence raised,
  no open cell closed, and the evidence itself is untouched (33/33 and 13/13
  digests still verify; both original manifests byte-unchanged). No required
  action remains open, every applicable challenge dimension carries a
  disposition, and independence is met under the reading disclosed above, so
  `accept` is permitted.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict passes the **adversarial gate only**,
  for `INV-01-09`, `INV-01-13`, and `INV-01-14`. It establishes no macOS
  behavior and no finding of its own, and closes no kill assumption: `KA-08`
