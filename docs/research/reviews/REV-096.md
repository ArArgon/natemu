## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: These are strong records and the adversarial pass confirms most of
  what they claim. The dispositions are bounded where they should be: `KA-01` is
  "not falsified on this cell" with its signing quadrant attached, `KA-09` is
  scoped to its build/test/debug limb with profiling conceded open, `KA-02` is
  decided documentarily and explicitly recorded as unaffected by a successful
  evidence run, and the `F3` input is one host. The gate rows are honest in the
  direction that costs the author. The licence audit's conditional structure,
  three use modes, and legal-uncertainty statement are better practice than the
  question required. I attacked the `F5` kill hardest and could not defeat it:
  `KA-02` as phrased is false, and the nearest counterexample — Rosetta for
  Linux running x86-64 user-mode binaries inside an arm64 guest — is
  translation, not direct hypervisor execution, and cannot host the unmodified
  full-system guest this envelope requires. `accept` is nonetheless prohibited
  by findings that bear on how these results travel onward. The licence audit's
  two-model set omits a copyleft-licensed NatEmu — an ordinary choice for an
  emulator, and precisely the model under which the table's only two
  "Incompatible" cells become compatible — so the `KA-10` family-kill surface is
  overstated and the record reaches for a design constraint where a licensing
  choice would do. The `F5` kill is routed through the weakest of family-kill
  item 1's four limbs while the register's own project-stop vocabulary fits
  better, and a family kill entering synthesis should not rest on an unstated
  reading. And the artifact carries two numbers that invite misreading
  downstream: `setrlimit` values that reflect this agent harness rather than
  macOS (default 256, not 1048576) and that exceed the kernel's own
  `kern.maxfilesperproc` cap of 61440, and a 64-vCPU framework ceiling recorded
  on a 10-core host. Each required change is small, concrete, and checkable, and
  none of them disturbs a result: they bound conclusions rather than withdraw
  them.
- Applicability limitations: This review challenges `INV-01-01`, `INV-01-10`,
  and `INV-01-11` only, at revision `891fb03`. It establishes no platform
  capability, no architecture selection, no legal conclusion, no performance
  result, and no synthesis outcome. Everything it accepts remains bounded to
  `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (`25G83`) / SDK 26.4 / CLT
  `26.4.0.0.1774242506`, ad-hoc signing, and non-Hardened-Runtime builds, with
  `P1` (M1 Pro / Sequoia 15) replication owed before any platform-wide use. The
  `KA-02`/`F5` disposition is documentary and architecture-wide by its own
  reasoning, not by observation. The prior-involvement limit disclosed above is
  material: this reviewer previously accepted the source, method, and code gates
  for these records, so a fresh adversarial reviewer remains worth commissioning
  before synthesis relies on them. Nothing here authorizes planning or
  implementation.
