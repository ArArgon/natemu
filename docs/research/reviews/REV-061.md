## Verdict

- Verdict: `accept`
- Rationale: all twelve required changes are closed, and the closures that
  matter most were verified by execution rather than by reading. The
  no-callback fix is the clearest case: the exact crafted output that failed
  under `REV-047` now passes, and an internally inconsistent output still
  fails, so the checker was narrowed to a structural invariant rather than
  simply loosened. The `RLIMIT_AS` schema fix is the second: the same `EINVAL`
  this reviewer observed before is now accompanied by a prior soft and hard
  limit of `RLIM_INFINITY` and an unchanged read-back, which excludes two of
  the three competing readings from the raw artifact itself. The attribution
  control is real code with its own signing, verification, and process-status
  capture, not a promise deferred to replication. The `BP-00-end` answer went
  past the minimum the action asked for and chose the branch that keeps `KA-13`
  about the mechanism actually deployed. And where the author could not close
  an action by measurement — the thread-summation premise — the record says so
  in the claim, the Confounders, the Method, and the Limitations rather than
  quietly upgrading the premise. No required action remains open, every
  applicable challenge dimension carries a disposition, and independence is met
  under the reading disclosed above, so `accept` is permitted.

  `INV-01-09` and `INV-01-14`, and expressly leaves `INV-01-13`'s method gate
  `not-applicable` rather than passing or failing it. It approves no source
