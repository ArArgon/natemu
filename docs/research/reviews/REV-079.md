## Verdict

- Verdict: `accept`
- Rationale: the fifteen `REV-044` and two `REV-064` required changes are all
  closed. The chain's substantive method defects — a cross-thread latency
  resting on an unstated clock-comparability assumption, a proportional
  overshoot with no control to separate host behaviour from the probe's own
  arithmetic, an aggregate multi-vCPU rate the guest requirement names but no
  arm measured, a threshold section that could print MEET beside an impossible
  value, undeclared thresholds, and a strengthened-arm zero read as
  corroboration without any test of whether the arms were comparable — are
  each closed by a control the method now carries and the checker now
  enforces, and this reviewer exercised each one. The two `d8cb40c` fixes are
  minimal and touch neither probe source. `accept` is permitted because no
  required action remains open and every applicable dimension is
  dispositioned.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict covers the methods of `INV-01-07`
  and `INV-01-08` at `d8cb40c` only. It approves no source claim, code, raw
  result, or replication, and it does not by itself authorize an evidence run:
  the source gate must pass first, and `REV-078` leaves it open on one ledger
  row. Every number quoted from the reviewer's runs is a review-validation
  observation on `Mac16,10` / macOS 26.6.2 (25G83) / SDK 26.4 at reduced
  sample counts under concurrent load; it is not evidence, it is not under the
