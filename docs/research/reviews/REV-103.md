## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The record survives adversarial reading better than any subject
  this reviewer has examined in this track, and the attacks that carried earlier
  rounds no longer land. The vacuity attack — the strongest one available
  against a prototype whose oracles are largely zero-valued — fails: four of the
  five zero-expected assertions are chained to a positive assertion that proves
  the test ran, and the fifth is already declared unwitnessed, while `cli_delta`
  survives quantitatively as well as structurally, since roughly 13 to 21 ticks
  would have landed inside the guest's `cli` region had `IF` been ignored and
  zero were observed in ten repetitions across two runs. The concurrency finding
  cannot be produced by a single core. The simplifications hold, including the
  two self-policing ones that convert into a `#GP` the checker fails on, and the
  one real dependency — that the faulting-push restart is observable only
  because `#PF` uses an IST stack — is declared, limited and routed. The `KA`
  dispositions follow from what was observed and stop there, with `KA-06`
  explicitly not upgraded because its stochastic control missed in both runs,
  and the family boundary refuses inference about `F3` in both directions. The
  record answers `Q-01-12` as the track's prototype clause requires and chooses
  no design. What blocks `accept` is not the evidence but the cross-record state
  it feeds: the kill-assumption register in `INV-01-02` — the register the
  track's exit criteria depend on — still reports `KA-11` as unestablished with
  no evidence run and three gates in `revise`, every clause of which is now
  false. Leaving that uncorrected would let synthesis read the register and the
  investigation as contradicting each other, or read the register alone and miss
  the finding entirely. It is a one-row correction, and it must carry the
  this-host bound rather than discharging an assumption phrased over the plural
  supported hosts. `accept` is prohibited while it and `REV-101`'s open action
  remain unresolved.
- Applicability limitations: This review challenges the `INV-01-12` record and
  the cross-record state its dispositions feed. It accepts no gate; it confirms
  no result beyond what `REV-101` reviewed and `REV-102` replicated. Everything
  it clears is bounded to `Mac16,10` (Apple M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (25G83) /
  SDK 26.4, ad-hoc signing, two vCPUs, one synthetic guest, five repetitions in
  each of two runs on one machine. It establishes no macOS API fact, no guest
  compatibility, no device, boot, performance or economic result, no family
  admissibility, and no synthesis outcome, and it selects none of `stop`,
  `continue targeted research`, or `authorize planning`. Nothing here authorizes
  planning or implementation.
