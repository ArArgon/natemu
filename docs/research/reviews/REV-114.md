  destructive check this review could bring against it. All five capture
  digests recompute to the ledger's capture-manifest values, 5/5, with no

  the entitlement question **is** limited to what the page says — the word
  "restricted" occurs zero times in the capture, the whole extracted prose
  contains no grant or restriction language in any wording, and no claim reads

  direction, since every step beyond the captures is labelled Inferred or
  Hypothesis. `accept` is nonetheless prohibited, and four required actions are
  open. Two are applicability overstatements: `CLM-01-02-04`'s cell promotes a
  sample-article prerequisite and a Fedora download-path token to "the
