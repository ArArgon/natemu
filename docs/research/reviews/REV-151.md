## Required changes

1. **R1 — INV-01-02 owner; return gate: adversarial.** Make the construction
   rule, labels, and closure accounting consistent. CLM-01-02-07 excludes
   code-generation-timing-only differences with the same platform surface;
   F4 expressly has F2's identical surface and same falsifiers, yet counts
   as a separate family. Preserve identifiers, but distinguish named
   overlapping candidates from true equivalence classes/variants or justify a
   revised distinctness rule and apply it uniformly to all sixteen cells.
   The privileged-helper cells can be dispositioned directly against the
   accepted deployment boundary; do not claim every helper requires exactly
   its twin's platform surface or satisfies neither distinctness limb without
   support. Recount any affected denominators and state what their count
   actually means. No candidate must be discarded to preserve six labels.
2. **R2 — INV-01-02 owner; return gate: source if adding documentary claims,
   then adversarial.** Separate required mechanisms from proposed/optional
   ones, especially in F3. The row inherits *everything in F2*, making host
   MAP_JIT/toggle availability a necessary dependency, and the interpreter×HVF
   cell then inherits the same kill set. An interpreter generates no code;
   moreover the primary hv_vm_map contract assigns permissions in the guest
   address space separately from host-process execution. The existing packet
   establishes neither that all F3 variants need host-executable JIT memory
   nor that they do not. Challenge the necessity, retain the candidate as an
   explicitly untested variant where appropriate, and prevent KA-01 failure
   from killing variants without an established dependency. Likewise mark
   stage-1 guest faults reaching VM exits as a proposed path, not an observed
   or documentary guarantee. Route the necessary mapping, exception routing,
   page-table mirroring/invalidation and SMP composition gaps to existing
   Q-01-01/Q-01-02/Q-02-03 and relevant shared-mapping questions in the backlog.
   A hypothesis with honest conditional kill routing can close this repair;
   an experiment or positive F3 finding is not required by this review.
3. **R3 — INV-01-02 owner; return gate: adversarial.** Correct all operative
   falsifiers to the policy they implement. Falsifier 3 calls both failures
   item 1 while routing the granule limb through item 2; name the appropriate
   limb and require failure of a necessary mechanism for the scoped variant.
   Falsifier 4's result-gated failure in two signing forms is insufficient by
   itself: require an eligible independently reviewed finding, applicable
   replication/adversarial gates, exact mandatory-cell scope, and failure of
   every relevant alternative for the proposed family kill. Reflect R2 in
   its F3 consequence. Falsifier 6 currently kills a family merely for
   required GPL linkage under *a model the project selects*; Model C is an
   immediate counterexample. Require actual incompatibility with the
   accepted model/use, and retain the current all-candidate-model analysis
   while distribution is undecided. GPL linkage alone is no kill.
4. **R4 — INV-01-02 owner; return gate: adversarial; route any policy change
   to Q-00-05/Q-00-08.** Repair the privileged-helper ordering derivation.
   INV-00-08 says a privileged-service dependency reopens the deployment
   boundary and preserves alternatives; INV-00-05 gives a no-admissible-family
   sufficient trigger. The latter does not by itself make that trigger
   exclusive. Reopening a gated question also does not relax the boundary,
   so argument 3's claim that reverse ordering silently widens scope is
   invalid. Either reconcile those accepted clauses without that inference
   or record the administrative ordering as unresolved with its existing
   backlog owner. The helper variant remains inadmissible under the retained
   boundary until a separate accepted change; open ordering need not become
   a claim that another family is killed or that the product boundary changed.
5. **R5 — INV-01-02 owner; return gate: adversarial.** Finish the internal
