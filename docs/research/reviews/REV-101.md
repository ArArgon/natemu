- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The evidence run is in unusually good order and almost everything
  in it survives independent re-derivation. All 48 manifest digests recompute
  and match, with no unlisted file and no row without a file; `sha256sums.txt`
  verifies against its own 39 entries; the environment capture is complete and
  matches the manifest's Environment table; and — the check that matters most —
  the four source digests recorded in `env.txt` are byte-identical to
  `git show 22d8516:`, so the run demonstrably used the revision the code gate
  accepted rather than a later tree that merely diffs empty. I traced every
  Observed number in the record to the raw field it came from across all five
  repetitions: 29 of 31 guest counters are identical in 5/5, the fault logs are
  byte-identical, the parallelism ratio recomputes to 2.00 in every run against
  the 1.50 floor, and every specific figure the record quotes — 73 and 38 TLB
  fills, 135 interrupts in repetition 1, 197 078 with 2 922 lost updates,
  1 034 312 blocks from 117 translations, the 178–250 ms wall range — is exactly
  what the artifact says. The relabelling is disciplined: the twelve claims
  whose predictions were met are Observed, and the one whose stochastic control
  missed is retained as **Hypothesis (not established)** with the consequence
  carried into `KA-06`, into the correctness risks, and into the backlog row
  rather than buried. Wall time and the ratio are both barred from performance
  use in terms; the KA and family dispositions are bounded to this host and to
  what was observed; the unentitled arm is reported without being turned into a
  `KA-01` finding. One required action nonetheless blocks `accept`: two Observed
  figures for vCPU 1 are stated as constants when the artifact shows them
  varying across the five repetitions. It is a traceability defect rather than a
  result defect — nothing depends on either number — but a result gate exists to
  certify that every Observed figure is the artifact's, and these two are not as
  written.
