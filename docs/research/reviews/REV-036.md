- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: the capture set is intact and most narrow statements accurately
  reflect admissible primary material, but several claims either contradict
  their cited revision, omit a controlling architectural condition, cite an

  correctness-relevant. Applicable challenge dimensions therefore have open
  required actions, so `accept` is prohibited.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict covers only the recovered source
  claims and mappings in the five named investigations at the recorded
  working-tree hashes. It reviews no experiment method, code, raw result,
  replication, performance sample, adversarial finding, synthesis outcome,
