## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The records identify many important confounders and correctly avoid declaring a current performance or feasibility result. They nevertheless leave required changes in baseline semantics/currency, fair tuning and device controls, statistical coverage and aggregation, SMP/platform coverage, complete result disposition, family enumeration, maturity, extension, and stop-rule logic. An `accept` verdict is prohibited while those actions remain open.
- Applicability limitations: The candidate rules have no established applicability beyond their stated proposed cells. They cannot support an all-host, all-family, SMP, economic, project-stop, continue-targeted-research, authorize-planning, or implementation conclusion.
