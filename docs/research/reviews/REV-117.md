## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: all three `REV-085` required changes are closed, and two of them
  are closed more thoroughly than asked. The Provisional resolution is
  genuinely rewritten rather than patched: it now separates what the run
  measured from what it did not and states outright that the store-to-load
  direction — the whole of the ordering divergence the question exists to
  probe — has no experimental support in this record, which is a harder thing
  to write than the sentence I asked for. The positive-control margin is
  recorded with both runs' figures and three binding consequences. The uncited
  variance band is withdrawn in every place it appeared, with the withdrawal
  itself explained, and the `T4b` miss now stands plainly with the
  replication's 66.27× quoted as corroboration that it is real. No manifest or
  raw artifact was touched. `accept` is prohibited by a single inconsistency
  the return itself introduced: the Results table's new INDETERMINATE verdict
  was not carried into `CLM-01-08-09`'s status cell, so the claim inventory
  and the results now disagree about one pair. The substantive conclusion does
  not change — both verdicts license nothing — but the claim inventory is what
  travels downstream.
- Applicability limitations: this verdict covers the `INV-01-08` record at
  `21dc5e5` and the artifacts `MAN-01-08-01` and `MAN-01-08-02`. Every Observed
  figure remains valid for `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (25G83) / SDK 26.4
  only, on a shared host. Nothing here disposes `KA-06`, establishes that the
  architecture forbids any outcome, or transfers to `P1`, where the Arm
  feature level differs and replication remains owed. This review approves no
  synthesis use, no planning, and no implementation.
