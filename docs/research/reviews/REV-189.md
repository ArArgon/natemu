## Correction disposition

R1 author's checklist is addressed by an operative six-row table. P uses
normal-return handlers without NODEFER; fault classes are masked during its
recoverable handler. D uses separately installed sacrificial dispositions:
both fault classes have NODEFER and neither appears in any D sa_mask, so
same-class BUS-in-BUS or SEGV-in-SEGV is explicitly requested, not assumed to
become the other signal. USR1/USR2 remain controlled; the controller waits for
the outer entry before sending USR2. Base masks, inherited-mask union and
setup readback are explicit. uc_sigmask is correctly treated as the interrupted
context mask, not the just-entered handler mask; no handler API is added.

Positive depth remains at most2. Terminal D depth is parent+1, up to3; depth3
never returns, repairs or injects again. Wrong depth/parent exits fail-closed
without claiming denial. Descriptors separate original R and W/X event
nonces, PC/address/operation, configuration, worker, phase and parent linkage.
Witness words, immutable stubs and atomic trace/ack slots have distinct
worker/event/depth ownership, avoiding simultaneous shared-word races. A
simultaneous denial child cannot exit successfully until every required
terminal record is acknowledged; unexpected access completion is a candidate
failure, not an endless wait. Same-class depth2/3 non-JIT preflights, wrong
identity/replay/depth and blocked-delivery controls are explicit. Missing,
blocked, crash or partial traces cannot become denial success.

The correction also records an important limitation: D's masks differ from
P's. Complementary permission results stay configuration-qualified and cannot
prove P has identical permission behavior. P's allowed-only lifecycle remains
separate. PROT_NONE process mapping phases expect both accesses denied rather
than attributing them to a per-thread JIT transition.

R2 is only partly addressed. The method pins accepted Q18 M18-2 and reconciles
post-ACK E/V reload/version floors, no artificial RESUME there, I hazards and
valid superseded calls, S/M STOP/RESUME, V's no-withheld-ACK rule, one outstanding
epoch and coordinator ownership through final-check/install or invalidation
ACK drain. It adds both final-check/install schedules and preserves retained
descriptor versus execution-right distinctions. Q18 code/validation/results
remain prerequisites, not supplied by method180. Delivered Mach baseline and
the full M-lane supplement remain absent here; work elsewhere cannot be
silently treated as accepted. Full INV19 method coverage/gate is still open.
