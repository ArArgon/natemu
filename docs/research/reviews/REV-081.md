## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: the evidence is in excellent shape and was verified rather than
  accepted. All 33 files of the `INV-01-09` artifact and all 13 of the
  `INV-01-14` artifact recompute correctly against both their own
  `SHA256SUMS.txt` and their manifests' tables — 46 of 46 — with no unlisted
  file and no manifest row lacking a file. Both `environment.txt` captures
  match the stated host exactly (`Mac16,10`, `hw.memsize 17179869184`,
  `hw.ncpu 10`, `hw.pagesize 16384`, macOS 26.6.2 build `25G83`, Apple clang
  21.0.0), and the accepted code revision was confirmed byte-identical to the
  code that ran. Every Observed number in both records traces to the raw
  output: `status_code: 1001` (`VMNET_FAILURE`) with `success: false` in both
  checkable arms; `socket_raw` `EPERM`; the full pty chain; `getentropy` 256
  succeeding and 257 refused with `EINVAL`; `st_blocks_before_punch: 8 →
  st_blocks_after_punch: 0`; `is_sparse: true` at `st_size: 104857600`;
  `emfile_observed: true` with `errno_at_failure: 24`; `sigxcpu_delivered:
  true`; `set_errno: 22` for both `RLIMIT_AS` and `RLIMIT_DATA` with the
  read-back limits unmoved; and `region_count 57 → 65`. The record's honesty
  under pressure is the strongest thing here: an errno miss reported as a
  miss, a predeclared outcome that did not occur left as open Hypothesis
  rather than quietly dropped, an open question resolved negatively and
  reported as a confirmed *unavailability*, and an alternative explanation
  narrowed rather than declared eliminated. `accept` is nevertheless
  prohibited, because one required action is open: a stated MiB figure that
  does not equal the byte count it glosses. It is a small error in a large
  and otherwise exact body of work, and it is exactly the kind a result gate
  exists to catch.
- Applicability limitations: this review judges the two evidence runs and the
  Observed relabels only. It approves no replication (`REV-082` covers that),
  no adversarial finding (`REV-083`), and no synthesis outcome. Every result
  it verifies is bounded to `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (`25G83`) /
  `MacOSX26.4.sdk`, ad-hoc-signed, unsandboxed, `uid=501`, single run, with
  `P1` replication owed before any platform-wide use; `vmnet` coverage is host
  mode only. It establishes no `KA-08`, `KA-12`, or `KA-13` closure, no
  machine-contract admissibility, no performance result, and authorizes no
  planning and no implementation.
