ready, C11 ready/go/abort synchronization,250000 protected increments each,
fresh128-aligned256-byte allocation, offset78/width4 and adjacent fixed

lock/unlock and actual-write counters are distinct.

normal F oracle independently recomputes wide integer tick-delta conversion
