- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: Both defects `REV-021` left open are verifiably closed — the NIST captures now exist, hash exactly as cited in every investigation, ledger row, and manifest entry, and their retained text genuinely supports the statistical claims including the Bonferroni page; and the rollover rule now covers every newer upstream stable release, major included, with the stale-data fallback intact. Retesting rather than deferring to the predecessor nevertheless produced three new adversarial findings that prohibit acceptance: the Apple capture, though hash-matching, is a JavaScript shell containing none of the sections `SRC-017` cites, so the artifact excluding Apple’s translation from the decision aggregate cannot be reproduced or attacked by a challenger; the repaired rollover trigger does not say whether “newer” means version order or publication date across five concurrently maintained QEMU stable series, leaving one stale-baseline path open; and `INV-00-06`’s source-gate row credits a review whose recorded scope excludes it. Under the critique template and evidence-policy gate completion, `accept` is prohibited while any required action is unresolved.
- Applicability limitations: No decision rule reviewed here establishes that QEMU executes any required cell, that a candidate is faster, correct, or economic, that any architecture family is killed, or that any project outcome is selected. Verification extends only to the retained 2026-09-03 captures and the current working-tree records, not to upstream pages as they stand now. Every future method, code, result, replication, and synthesis obligation remains outstanding under its own gate.
