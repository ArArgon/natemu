## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The integrity layer is sound and the hardest citations hold. All
  14 Track 01 captures and all 10 cited SDK headers reproduce their recorded
  SHA-256 digests, 24/24, with no substituted file among them; every cited
  header line number is correct; and the two claims this review was directed to
  attack hardest are literally present in the captured Apple rendition when its
  `text` and `codeVoice` nodes are concatenated in document order — the
  one-`MAP_JIT`-region sentence (`CLM-01-04-06`) and the jit-write-allowlist
  sentence (`CLM-01-04-07`) are verbatim, correctly scoped to the
  hardened-runtime configuration, and not reconstructed from memory. The
  hardened-runtime enumeration (`CLM-01-04-11`) is confirmed structurally in the
  page's own "Runtime Exceptions" topic section, and both Intel SDM quotations
  are verbatim at the cited extraction lines of the June 2026 Order 253668
  volume. `accept` is nevertheless prohibited: four Source-backed or
  Source-derived claims are not supported by the sections cited, and one of them
  is refuted by its own source and by the record's own instrument.
  `CLM-01-03-02` cites a flag (`MAP_NORESERVE`) that does not occur in the
  `mmap(2)` page at all — the very defect the record correctly identifies for
  `MADV_FREE_REUSABLE` one investigation later; `CLM-01-03-05` asserts that
  `mincore(2)` does not report modification while the cited DESCRIPTION defines
  `MINCORE_MODIFIED` and the probe's own output returns it set, and on that
  false basis closes a tracking candidate that `BP-01-05-start` had expressly
  left open; `CLM-01-06-04` calls the alternate signal stack per-thread on a
  page that never says "thread"; and `CLM-01-06-06` presents a compile-time
  header property as an observation about delivered state. Separately, the
  16 KiB granularity on which `KA-07` and every dependent family screening rest
  is asserted without a label, without a source, and generalized to "supported
  hosts" from a single-host Hypothesis. Six required changes are open, so
  `revise` is the only permitted verdict; each names its record, its section,
  and its return gate.
- Applicability limitations: This review decides the source gate for
  `INV-01-03`, `INV-01-04`, `INV-01-05`, and `INV-01-06` and nothing else. It
  establishes no method acceptance, no code acceptance, no experimental result,

  outcome. Its digest verification is bounded to the 2026-09-03 capture date and
