## Verdict

- Verdict: `accept`
- Rationale: The return repairs the central defect honestly rather than by
  restating it. The record had defined "sustainable" as cross-release,
  public-distribution availability and then measured single-cell presence; it now
  scopes its claim to this cell, scopes the SDK-coexistence metric to
  "this-cell" evidence, and leaves the platform-wide claim owed to `P1`. The
  provenance gap that made this concrete is closed at the artifact level: the
  probe emits a per-tool `provenance` field, and `CLM-01-10-04` now states that
  `rustc` resolves `user-local` and `codesign` `OS` rather than under CLT, and
  that neither is by itself evidence of public distribution — I confirmed both
  values on a live run. `KA-09` is recorded as dispositioned for the
  build/test/debug limb only, with the profiling limb conceded open and routed,
  and that qualifier is carried consistently into the falsifier heading, the stop
  conditions, the metrics, and the replication gate row rather than stated once
  and forgotten. The checker threshold and the Metrics threshold now agree, which
  I verified by re-running the `clang`-absent injection that previously passed
  and now fails with a named `KA-09` rule. The `xctrace` test is tied to the
  resolved binary and its actual failure message is captured into the artifact,
  so the record's quoted reason is verifiable rather than asserted. The
  unexecutable exclusion is withdrawn with an explanation instead of being
  papered over with a plausible-sounding rule, and the Homebrew limb is scoped
  out rather than left as a falsifier the method cannot exercise. No required
  action remains open, every applicable dimension carries a disposition, and
  independence is met, so `accept` is permitted.
- Applicability limitations: This review accepts the method gate for
  `INV-01-10` and nothing else, at revision `6c0acf5`. It does not adjudicate
  `INV-01-11`, which proposes no experiment. It establishes no toolchain fact
  and no `KA-09` disposition — every inventory claim remains a Hypothesis and no
  evidence run has occurred; and any future `KA-09` disposition is limited to
  the build/test/debug limb on the cell measured, with the profiling limb open
  and `P1` replication owed. Tool paths and provenance values quoted above come
  from reviewer smoke and fault-injection runs in a scratch directory, are not
  evidence, and support no claim. This review does not accept the sources
  (`REV-069`) or the code (`REV-071`), and nothing here authorizes an evidence
  run, planning, or implementation.
