## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: The integrity work is sound and survives independent
  recomputation — three captures, three digests recomputed, 3/3 match against
  `source-ledger.md` lines 314–316; every cited line range exists and reads as
  the record quotes it, including `_PAGE_BIT_ACCESSED 5 /* was accessed (raised
  by CPU) */` and `_PAGE_BIT_DIRTY 6 /* was written to (raised by CPU) */` at
  lines 15–16 and the `/* Return frame for iretq */` comment with `rip`, `cs`,
  `eflags`, `rsp`, `ss` in that order at lines 70–75; the merged ledger rows
  reproduce the subject's inventory without drift; no citation is from memory;
  no secondary source appears; and the dependency sources are genuinely
  unclaimed. Three defects nonetheless block `accept`. `CLM-01-12-01`'s cited
  line range under-covers the semantics it asserts and adds an unsourced
  "(present)" gloss. `CLM-01-12-03` is made the source authority for what the
  prototype's own emitter pushes, when the applicable primary specification —
  Intel SDM Vol. 3A §7.14.2 — is already in the capture store with registered
  digests and, on inspection, contradicts the emitter on the 16-byte RSP
  alignment that no declared simplification covers. `CLM-01-12-02` cites A/D
  bits that the experiment sets but never observes, in the face of a breadth
  pass that named A/D as a required observable. `accept` is prohibited while
  those five actions are open.
- Applicability limitations: This review judges claim-level source support for
  `CLM-01-12-01`–`CLM-01-12-03` only, bounded by the 2026-09-03 capture date
  and the `v6.12` and June-2026 revisions inspected. It establishes no macOS
  API fact, no prototype behaviour, no `KA-04`, `KA-06`, or `KA-11`
  disposition, no family judgment, and no synthesis outcome, and it accepts no
  other gate for `INV-01-12`. Changing a cited revision or capture reopens it.
