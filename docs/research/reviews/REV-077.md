## Required changes

1. `INV-01-05`, `CLM-01-05-14` and the family-implication paragraph (lines
   405–412): withdraw `mincore`'s `MINCORE_MODIFIED` from the list of tracking
   mechanisms, or re-file it as an explicitly unevaluated candidate. What was
   observed (`CLM-01-03-14`) is one set-then-clear transition on a 64 KiB
   region, single-threaded, with no cost, no clearing granularity, and no
   concurrent-safety measurement. Three specific gaps must be recorded if the
   candidate is kept: that the only observed clearing mechanism,
   `MADV_FREE_REUSABLE`, is a memory-reclaim advisory ("pages can be reused (by
   anyone)", `sys/mman.h:217`, sitting between `MADV_FREE` "discard contents"
   and `MADV_FREE_REUSE`) and so may reclaim live guest RAM if used to re-arm
   tracking; that it is undocumented in `madvise(2)` by the record's own
   `CLM-01-05-06` and its admissibility is therefore an open `Q-01-01`
   question; and that per-poll cost over a guest-sized region is unmeasured and
   belongs to `Q-04-01`. Correspondingly, "at least three viable mechanisms" in
   the family paragraph must become two measured mechanisms plus one
   unevaluated candidate. Owner: `t01_memory_author`. Return gate: adversarial.
2. `INV-01-05`, `CLM-01-05-14`, and every family-implication paragraph that
   reasons from a cost figure: remove the quantitative qualifiers or mark them
   non-reproducible. `CLM-01-05-14`'s "protection faults, ~4.8 µs each" and the
   "cheap enough that it is not obviously excluded" reasoning rest on figures
   that `REV-076` re-measured at −52.9% to +631% on the same host and binary.
   The dispositions may keep their mechanism basis; they may not keep an
   unqualified magnitude. Closing `REV-075` action 3 and `REV-076` action 1
   together with this one is sufficient; this action exists so the adversarial
   gate does not pass while a synthesis reader could still lift "~4.8 µs" out of
   a kill-assumption claim. Owner: `t01_memory_author`. Return gate: adversarial.

## Verdict

- Verdict: `revise`
- Rationale: Attacked at the resolution level rather than the artifact level,
  these records hold up well on everything that decides feasibility and less
  well on one inference and on their numbers. The falsifiers that would have
  hurt were genuinely live and did not fire — the sub-host-page `mprotect` was
  rejected rather than silently rounded, a written PC and register were
  honored, and a thread executed from the code cache while another provably
  held write access — and all three reproduced exactly under independent
  re-execution. Three of the four kill-assumption dispositions are models of
  bounded inference: `KA-03` "not falsified on this host" and expressly not a
  confirmation, `KA-01` scoped to the ad-hoc cell with the Developer ID cell
  named as untested and the hardened-runtime single-region rule named as the
  binding constraint on any shipped design, `KA-04` claiming only the host half
  of restartability. The family implications say "not killed", never
  "admissible", and route every design question to a named owner; SMP is
  bounded to two threads throughout; and no resolution chooses a mechanism.
  `accept` is prohibited on two findings. First, `KA-07`'s disposition recruits
  a third tracking mechanism — `mincore`'s `MINCORE_MODIFIED` — that was never
  measured as a mechanism: one set-then-clear observation on four pages, whose
  only observed re-arm is an undocumented memory-reclaim advisory that may
  discard the guest memory it is meant to track, in a record that elsewhere
  treats unnoticed guest writes as producing "wrong answers rather than
  crashes". That inference is load-bearing in the `F2`/`F4` paragraph and is not
  carried by its evidence. Second, the cost magnitudes quoted inside
  `CLM-01-05-14` and reasoned from in the family paragraphs are not
  reproducible: the same binary on the same host moved them by −52.9% to +631%.
  Neither finding overturns a disposition — both `KA-07` mechanisms that were
  actually measured are sound, and the tracking result reproduced exactly — but
  both let a downstream reader take more from these records than the evidence
  gives. Two required changes are open.
- Applicability limitations: This review challenges the Track 01 memory and
  exception findings only, and accepts nothing: the adversarial gate for
  `INV-01-03`, `INV-01-04`, `INV-01-05`, and `INV-01-06` does not pass on this
  verdict. It establishes no platform behavior, no cost, no family
  admissibility, no guest-correctness property, and no synthesis outcome.
  Everything it examines is bounded to `Mac16,10` (M4) / macOS 26.6.2 (`25G83`)
  / SDK 26.4 / Apple clang 21.0.0 / ad-hoc signing with no hardened runtime, at
  1 and 2 threads with uncontrolled placement; `P1` replication, the Developer
  ID / hardened-runtime / notarized cell, the ptrauth-enabled build cell, 4- and
  8-vCPU scaling, and workload-shaped access patterns all remain owed. The
  independence limit disclosed above — that this reviewer cleared the source,
  method, and code gates these findings rest on — bounds the confidence of a
  future `accept` here, and a later adversarial reviewer without that prior
  involvement would still add value. Nothing here authorizes planning or
  implementation.
