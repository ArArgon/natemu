## Outcome: integrity FAIL

`check.txt` records `RESULT: FAIL (1 integrity failures)`. Seventeen of the
eighteen integrity rules passed; `I16` failed:

> `I16 timebase arithmetic agrees with an independent clock`: same-thread arms
> whose mach-arithmetic error disagrees with an independent `CLOCK_UPTIME_RAW`
> elapsed measurement by more than 10% of the target:
> `[('mach_wait_until', 100000, 'background', 16875)]`

Because an integrity rule failed, `check.py` suppressed the whole threshold
section, so this artifact reports **no** `T1`–`T4` verdict, and under
`INV-01-07`'s frozen stop criteria the run produces no claim. The
predeclared `T2` disposition is therefore **not** triggered: `T2` was never
evaluated, so there is no miss to route.

Diagnosis, recorded as the diagnosis of a method failure and **not** as an
Observed claim about the host: `I16` fired on exactly one of the twelve
enforced same-thread arms, and by 16 875 ns against a 10 000 ns tolerance —
the tolerance being 10% of the 100 µs target, the smallest of the three. The
same arm at user-interactive QoS disagreed by 333 ns, and `nanosleep` at the
same target and QoS by 1 959 ns. The metric is a worst case over 2000 samples.
The control brackets a slightly wider interval than the measurement it checks:
`ctrl0` is read before `mach_absolute_time()` is read for the deadline, so a
deschedule in that window is counted in the control's elapsed time but not in
the mach-arithmetic error. A ~17 µs deschedule of a background-QoS thread is
ordinary. The evidence therefore points at a **bracket mis-specification in
the control**, not at a timebase-conversion defect — but that is a diagnosis
to be adjudicated at the method gate, not a conclusion this manifest may draw,
and the alternative that the arithmetic really does disagree is not excluded
by this artifact.

No threshold was loosened and no re-run was attempted. `INV-01-07` fixes its
thresholds in `check.py` before any run and forbids changing one after data
exists without reopening the method gate; adjusting `I16` now, or re-running
until it passed, would be precisely the post-hoc manoeuvre the method exists
to prevent.
