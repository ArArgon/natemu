## Reproduction outcome

- **Every delivery and restart claim reproduced exactly; the latency
  magnitude did not.**
- `CLM-01-06-06` reproduced: `__DARWIN_OPAQUE_ARM_THREAD_STATE64` 0,
  `ptrauth_calls` 0, 4-byte `__esr`, and — the conjunct that is an actual
  observation — `uc_mcsize` 816 on every delivered fault, equal to the
  compiled `sizeof(_STRUCT_MCONTEXT64)`.
- `CLM-01-06-07` and `CLM-01-06-09` reproduced exactly: all three fault
  classes delivered with the faulting PC inside the stub, a non-zero stack
  pointer, a matching `__far` for the memory case, distinct signals
  (`SIGSEGV` 11, `SIGILL` 4, `SIGTRAP` 5), and each resumed to the declared
  sentinel.
- `CLM-01-06-11` reproduced exactly: each of two concurrent threads observed
  its own `__far` and resumed with its own value.
- `CLM-01-06-12` reproduced: every Mach exception-port call returned
  `KERN_SUCCESS`, one task-level `EXC_BAD_ACCESS` handler was already
  installed, and the probe again claims no delivery through the port.
- `CLM-01-06-10` **did not reproduce to the quoted precision**: median
  1375.0 ns against 2916.7 ns in the original, **−52.9%**, with 0 missed
  faults in both. The claim's own terms — single-digit microseconds, median
  under 10 µs — hold in both runs, but the figure is not stable to better
  than a factor of two on this host.

**Statement:** Every `INV-01-06` delivery, state-completeness, restart-fidelity, per-thread, and Mach-API result reproduced exactly. `CLM-01-06-10`'s latency reproduced in kind but not in magnitude: median 1375.0 ns against 2916.7, −52.9%.
