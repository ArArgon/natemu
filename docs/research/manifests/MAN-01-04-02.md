## Reproduction outcome

- **Every capability claim reproduced; one timing magnitude did not.**
- `CLM-01-04-12` reproduced: `pthread_jit_write_protect_supported_np()`
  returned 1 and all three `MAP_JIT` mappings (256 MiB, 1 GiB, 4 GiB) were
  obtained with `errno` 0.
- `CLM-01-04-14` reproduced: the stub returned its declared value 42 after
  `sys_icache_invalidate`.
- `CLM-01-04-15` reproduced **with its witness**: `witness_observed` equalled
  `witness_expected` and `writer_held_write_access` was true, so the writing
  thread demonstrably held write access while the other thread executed and
  returned 42. Without the witness this result would be vacuous (`REV-042`
  action 8).
- `CLM-01-04-16` reproduced: executing from the region while holding write
  access faulted (`SIGBUS` 10), and a write without toggling faulted
  (`SIGBUS` 10, `si_code` 1, `si_addr` in the region).
- `CLM-01-04-13` **did not reproduce to the quoted precision**: the batched
  per-pair median was 19.5000 ns here against 26.7500 ns in the original, −27.1%.
  The *claim* still holds — the figure is tens of nanoseconds, well under the
  record's 200 ns threshold, and below the 41.667 ns clock quantum, which
  confirms the nanosecond-domain division — but the specific median is not
  stable run to run. The predeclared precision target was met again
  (shortest batch 477 ticks originally, comfortably above the 100-tick target).

**Statement:** Every `INV-01-04` capability result reproduced. `CLM-01-04-13`'s median reproduced in kind (tens of nanoseconds, below the clock quantum) but not in magnitude: 19.50 ns/pair against 26.75, −27.1%.
