## Reproduction outcome

- **Every soundness and granularity claim reproduced exactly; every timing
  magnitude moved, one of them by a factor that matters.**
- `CLM-01-05-07` reproduced exactly: 2000 protected pages written produced
  2000 recorded faults, all 2000 addresses inside the tracked region.
- `CLM-01-05-10` attribution reproduced exactly: 1000 identity matches, 0
  mismatches, 0 faults without an identity. The **cost effect did not
  reproduce**: this run measured a concurrent-versus-single median difference
  of **+17.93%** against the record's +4.3% — a factor of 4.2 — recomputed
  independently by this reviewer's `verify.py`. The record's decision to
  withdraw the contention reading is confirmed as correct, and the +4.3%
  magnitude itself is shown to be unstable and must not be quoted.
- `CLM-01-05-11` reproduced exactly: `EINVAL` on the sub-host-page request,
  the host-page-aligned control succeeding, and the slot pattern
  `[F,F,F,F,T,T,T,T]`.
- `CLM-01-05-12` reproduced exactly: `SIGBUS` 10 / `si_code` 1 for the
  protection violation, `SIGSEGV` 11 / `si_code` 2 for the unmapped access.
- `CLM-01-05-13` reproduced: isolated marginal 0.0958 ns/op against 0.0859
  (+11.5%), sign-consistent across all 8 repetitions again, clearing a
  criterion that tightened to 0.0047 ns/op. This is the most robust timing
  figure in the group and vindicates the `REV-041` action 4 redesign.
- `CLM-01-05-08` reproduced in kind: median 6041.7 ns against 4833.3 (+25.0%),
  still low-single-digit microseconds — but the maximum went from 17875.0 ns
  to 130666.7 ns, **+631%**.
- `CLM-01-05-09` did not reproduce to precision: `mprotect` median 583.3 ns
  against 416.7 (+40.0%), still under the record's 1 µs threshold.
- `CLM-01-05-15` reproduced in direction: whole-loop marginal −0.3481 ns/store
  against −0.1585, still negative, still correctly marked not interpretable as
  a bitmap cost. The sign-consistency conjunct the record predicts is **not
  observable in this replication either**, because the probe emits sign
  consistency for the isolated pair only.

**Statement:** Every `INV-01-05` soundness, attribution, granularity, and signal-class result reproduced exactly, and `CLM-01-05-13` reproduced within 11.5%. Timing magnitudes did not reproduce: `CLM-01-05-10`'s cost effect moved from +4.3% to +17.93%, `CLM-01-05-09`'s median by +40.0%, and `CLM-01-05-08`'s maximum by +631%. `CLM-01-05-15`'s sign-consistency conjunct remains unobservable.
